Adjusting the Magnification of a Screen Image

  1. From the Window menu, choose the window containing the screen image for which you want to adjust the magnification.
  2. From the View menu, choose Zoom In to increase the magnification of the screen image.

    - or -

    From the View menu, choose Zoom Out to decrease the magnification of the screen image.
