Copying a Screen Image to the Clipboard

  1. From the Window menu, choose the window containing the screen image that you want to copy.
  2. From the Edit menu, choose Copy Window to copy the visible portion of the screen image to the Clipboard.

    - or -

    From the Edit menu, choose Copy All to copy the entire contents of the window to the Clipboard.
