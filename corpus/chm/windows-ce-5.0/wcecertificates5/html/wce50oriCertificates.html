Certificates

Authentication is crucial to secure communications. Users must be able to prove their identity to those with whom they communicate and must be able to verify the identity of others. Authentication of identity on a network is complex because the communicating parties do not physically meet as they communicate. This can allow an unethical person to intercept messages or to impersonate another person or entity. A method must be worked out to maintain the necessary level of trust within the communication process. Certificates enable users to provide enhanced security in communications by providing a common credential to verify identity.
