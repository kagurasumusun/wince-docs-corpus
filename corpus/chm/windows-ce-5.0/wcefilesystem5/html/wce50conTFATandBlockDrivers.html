TFAT and Block Drivers

TFAT is an extension of FATFS. TFAT requires a hardware-specific driver designed for the type of media on which the volume will exist. The driver abstracts the media storage and should provide atomic block operations. For flash storage, the block driver must provide a verification of atomicity on the block level to provide transaction-safety for data on a disk.
