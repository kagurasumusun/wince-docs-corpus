National Language Support (NLS)

Microsoft® Windows® CE supports the National Language Support (NLS) feature. NLS enables to support the different locale-specific needs of users around the world. NLS includes support for locale-specific keyboards and fonts. NLS support also enables applications to set the locale for the user. NLS also enables applications to retrieve and display strings representing times, dates, and other information formatted correctly for the specified language and location.
