VJFVON

When selected, matches minor grammatical variations of the word or phrase you entered, as well as the word or phrase itself. For example, if you entered “add” and selected this box, InfoViewer would find “add,” “adds,” and “added.” This option is independent of other options or syntax. If you do a titles-only search, variations in titles will be matched. If you use quotes (or any other query operator) any variation of the word can appear; for example "stemmed search" will also match "stemming search".
