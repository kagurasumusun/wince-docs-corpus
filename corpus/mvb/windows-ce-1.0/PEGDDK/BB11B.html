How The Device Manager Loads Devices

From the perspective of the Device Manager, all drivers are classified as built-in or installable. Figure 2 shows an abbreviated process for loading built-in and installable drivers. Detailed process flow diagrams for loading built-in and installable drivers appear later in this section.

Generally, built-in drivers are loaded as part of the boot process and are never unloaded. However, the Device Manager places no restrictions on a built-in driver that keep it from being unloaded. Not all built-in drivers are loaded by the Device Manager. For example, the touch screen and audio drivers are loaded by another part of the operating system.

For PC Card devices (currently, the only type of installable device supported), the Device Manager dynamically loads the appropriate driver DLL when a device is inserted into the PC Card socket. The Device Manager also unloads the PC Card driver when a device is removed from the PC Card socket. The block diagram assumes the PC Card slot is empty when the system starts up. The CE_CARD_INSERTION and CE_CARD_REMOVAL events are generated by the PC Card driver upon insertion or removal of a PC Card into/from the PC Card socket.
