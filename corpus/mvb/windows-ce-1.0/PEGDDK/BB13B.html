Loading PC Card Drivers

From the perspective of the Device Manager, the only installable device is a PC Card device. Serial type devices can be connected to a serial port, but the Device Manager does not respond to the event.

The Device Manager loads PC Card drivers using a process very similar to the process used for built-in drivers. The most significant change between the two processes is that the Device Manager must detect the type of device inserted into the PC Card slot, the device type is known for built-in drivers and detection is not required.
