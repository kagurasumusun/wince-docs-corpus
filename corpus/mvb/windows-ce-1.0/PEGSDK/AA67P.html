<P>

Indicates paragraphs. Note that technically there are both start and end tags which are supposed to surround all paragraphs, but that most people have never even seen a </p> tag, and frequently leave out the initial <p> tag.

ALIGN

left, right, center: Specifies the alignment for the paragraph occurring between the <p align=x> and </p> tags. This information has to be included in the paragraph header.

The spacing above and below the paragraph defaults at 12 pixels between the baseline and the ascender of the text (given that the default font is Arial 9pt). The spacing grows accordingly to the size of the font where the space equals a line and half (see also Hn).
