The Transport Service Structures

The transport service DLL primarily uses two structures: the SERVICE structure and the MailMsg structure.

SERVICE Structure

The SERVICE structure holds IDs for all messages, along with related information. This is the primary means of passing information to and from the outside world, and between various members of the DLL. A handle to this private SERVICE is passed back as the first parameter of the TransportInit function.

MailMsg Structure

You can access the actual contents of a message using the MailMsg structure. For the transport service, this structure is accessed via a handle that is passed to the Transport* functions. The MailMsg structure can also be accessed through the message store API. This structure contains pointers to the mail headers and message body, along with other related information. For more information on the Mail API, see the “Mail Interface” chapter.
