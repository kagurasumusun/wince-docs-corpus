Color

Windows CE currently supports only four gray-scale colors, black, white, light gray, and dark gray. However, even using this set, single pixel graphical elements such as dots or lines can be difficult to distinguish without an adjacent strong contrasting color. For example, white and light gray can be hard to see unless presented against a black or dark gray background. When using these lighter colors, you may also need to double the thickness of the pixels or lines.

It is best to limit your use of light gray to accent and anti-aliasing convention. Light gray works well as a surrounding contrast color where you have many or large controls with white backgrounds.

The World Clock, Alarms view (on light gray background)

When using light gray as the background color for your window, use a white line below the command bar to visually separate the command bar from the rest of the window.
