SH-3 Calling Standard

This document describes revision 1.4 of the NK/SH3 calling standard. Continuing development of this standard will require a cooperative effort between the compiler, operating system, and hardware architecture teams.

Several issues remain under review with the revision of this standard. They are detailed in the section, Issues.

It is a fundamental goal of this standard development effort to ensure that programs developed under older versions will be compatible with later revisions of the standard.
