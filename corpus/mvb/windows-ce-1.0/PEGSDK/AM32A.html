Layout

Design the layout of your windows to only include as many controls as are necessary and space them to provide sufficient area so that users can easily target them. For example, you should place controls no less than 4 pixels from the edge of the screen, unless they need to align with the left edge of the command bar, such as a tab control. You should also use a minimum of 2 pixels between controls. Use blank space or group box controls to group related controls.
