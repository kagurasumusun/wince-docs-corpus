<Hn>

Where n=1-6. Use H1 through H6 to specify different sizes and styles of heading. This will cause a change in the FONTINDEX, generating a new header. Headers will appear as bold text, flush left with paragraph breaks before and after a heading.. (see <p> where the default spacing equals 12 pixels between the base of the text above and the ascender below.)
