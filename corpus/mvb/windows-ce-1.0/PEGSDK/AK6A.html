Troubleshooting

Hey! When I build I get connection errors? What's up?

You may have the wrong enviroment selected. Check the default project workspace. Make sure it’s set to one of the emulation options.

Hey! I rebuilt my .exe and the IDE never gave me the option of where put my executable. What's up?

You may have to move your .exe file to the correct directory yourself. You can do this using the Remote Object Viewer. (Remeber to exit from the option to open a remote connection if you do not have a device.)

Hey! When I click on the emulated Start button I receive a "welcome exe not found" error. What's up?

You may have a corrupted or missing Pegfile.dat. Copy a new Pegfile.dat from your SDK CD-ROM to your \msdev\peg\emul directory and rebuild your application.
