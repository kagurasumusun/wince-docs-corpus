In general, %macro-name expands into a direct register reference such as $0 or $1. For example, the inline assembler expands %t0 into $8, as indicated by the preceding list. Macro names of the form number are also supported for all registers. For example, %r9 is expanded into $9.
You can also refer to the MIPS processor’s on-chip floating-point registers as $f0 through $f30. For a description of how the different floating-point registers are used, see the MIPS Assembly Language Programmer’s Guide.
