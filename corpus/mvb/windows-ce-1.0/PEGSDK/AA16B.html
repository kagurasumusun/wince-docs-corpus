Messaging and Notifications

Messaging works much the same in Windows CE as it does in Windows. Not all messages are supported however, particularly WM_* and EM_*. Some that are supported have been modified. Some of the most common messages, and how they differ from their Win32 equivalents, are listed in the Table 3.

Notification is also basically similar to what you will be familiar with in Win32, but Windows CE has some additional capabilities. See Chapter8, "Notifications," for further discussion.
