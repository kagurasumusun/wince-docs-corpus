Using Graphics

While one of the most appealing features of Windows is the rich graphical environment you can create, graphics can be very demanding in terms of memory. You may need to limit your use of bitmaps and other graphic displays in order to conserve memory. One approach might be to use an algorithm to generate an image, rather than simply loading a bitmap or other graphics file. This approach provides a speed versus memory tradeoff that may be attractive. It will probably work best for line drawings but may be practical for other types of graphics.
