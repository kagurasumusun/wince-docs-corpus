TerminateProcess

Terminates a process.

BOOL TerminateProcess(// winbase.h
HANDLE hProc,// handle of process
DWORD dwRetVal// ignored
);
Parameters
hProc
Handle of process (ProcessID).
dwRetVal
Return value of process (ignored).
Remarks
You cannot kill yourself (no suicide). You cannot kill a PSL. You may not be able to kill a process wedged inside a PSL.
