<BR>

The Line Break element specifies that a new line must be started at the given point. A new line indents the same as that of line-wrapped text.

Default line spacing is 6 pixels between the base of the text above and the ascender below.(6pt leading). Default right and left indent is 6 pixels or 1.25% of area.

CLEAR

With the addition of floating images, it was necessary to expand the <BR> element. Normal <BR> still just inserts a line break. A CLEAR attribute has been added to <BR>, so that:

CLEAR=left will break the line, and move vertically down until you have a clear left margin (no floating images).

CLEAR=right does the same for the right margin.

CLEAR=all moves down until both margins are clear of images.

Under certain circumstances, this will be the end of a re-layout. (eg. If we are relaying out after a left aligned image, a <br clear=left> will mean that we are done laying out.
