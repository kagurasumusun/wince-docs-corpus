Power Management

Introduction to Power Management

A Handheld PC (HPC) must actively manage its power consumption to extend the life of its limited batteries. As part of its power management strategy, the operating system automatically selects operating states to conserve power based on user and program activities.

All applications can contribute to power management by returning control to the operating system when possible to let it determine when the system is inactive. It can then select the most efficient operating state for the device. Most applications do not need any special techniques for effective power management; they can let the operating system manage the power.

Time-based applications, or applications that are affected if their execution is suspended are most affected by power management activities. This chapter describes how the different device operating states might affect some applications and what an application can do for efficient operation under the different power management operating modes.
