Integrating Windows CE Bundled Applications

Windows CE bundles a variety of applications for your use. These applications, while not part of the operating system, are often integral to any application's success on a given device. The following applications expose APIs you can use in developing your own applications.

  • Contacts Database
  • Windows CE Help
  • Input Method Editor
  • Spelling Checker.

This chapter discusses how to access the capabilities of the bundled applications, and how to integrate those APIs into your own applications.
