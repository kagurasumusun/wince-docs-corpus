Starting Emulation

    To Start Emulation

  1. Click Start, and point to Programs.
  2. Point to Windows CE Platform SDK.
  3. Click on the emulation option that corresponds to the platform you wish to emulate. For example, to emulate the Handheld PC, select Desktop HPC Emulation.

The emulation window is used to observe the behavior of Windows CE applications under development without having to download executables or have a remote device connected to your desktop PC.
