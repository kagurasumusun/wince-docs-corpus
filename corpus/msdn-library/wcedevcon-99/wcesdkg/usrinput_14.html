Recognizing a Hand-Drawn Character

The recognition of hand-drawn alpha-numeric characters and glyphs occurs within the boundaries of a box, or multiple of boxes, that you define for the input panel of your target device. The structure of the applicable language determines how multiple boxes are arranged, for example, from left to right and top to bottom for English. By moving a stylus in a predefined pattern within the box, the handwriting recognition engine employed by your application can process and recognize this input, one character or glyph at a time, to produce the corresponding Unicode output.
