Managing Power on a Mobile Platform

The primary function of power management is to increase the battery life of a Windows CE mobile platform. This is accomplished by providing accurate estimates of remaining battery life and notifying the user when the batteries are nearing depletion. Power management for a mobile platform is based on the following assumptions:

  • The platform is used less than two hours per day in bursts from five minutes to one hour.
  • The display is powered 100 percent of the time during use.
  • The platform runs less than 10 percent of the time during typical use.
  • The platform uses both main batteries and a backup battery.
  • The platform has no nonvolatile, writable memory.
  • Maximum battery life is obtained without PC Card or Flash storage devices or those that require very little power from the device batteries. Storage devices drawing appreciable power from the internal batteries significantly reduce battery life.
