In an effort to continue to meet customers changing needs and lead an evolution of technology, Compaq has conducted extensive market research and devoted significant resources to study customer requirements, alliances, hardware and software technology, competition and market entry strategies.

Testing Process

The stringent testing process guarantees the Compaq Aero 2100 will continue the tradition of high quality, reliable products from Compaq.

  • Communications testing assures easy setup and operation of communication to the desktop, and to other palm-size PCs. Testing includes modems, infrared, serial and network connections.

  • Compatibility testing assures interoperability with desktops, portables and workstations currently in development as well as those already announced. Testing is performed with both Compaq and non-Compaq products.
  • Performance and battery life testing guarantees superior performance and long lasting battery life.
  • Packaging and safety testing secure proper protection during handling, shipment and storage.

 

Service and Support

The Compaq Aero 2100 is protected under Compaq's generous support umbrella known as CompaqCare. The service and support package includes the following features:

  • Mail-in warranty limits customer cost, downtime and inconvenience by sending the customer a replacement unit within five days of a reported problem (North America and Canada only).

  • Carry-in warranty allows user to receive a replacement unit when a faulty product is carried in to a Compaq certified dealer. (ROW)
  • Round-the-clock support is available through Compaq's 24 x 7 technical support hotline.
  • On-line support forums are available on Prodigy, CompuServe, America Online, and the Internet. PaqFax information also offers ample opportunity to learn more about the product.
