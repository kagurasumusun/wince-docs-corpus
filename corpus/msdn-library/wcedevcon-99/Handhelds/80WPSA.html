ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS SAMPLER

Compaq understands the challenge of integrating mobile computing tools into complex enterprise systems. That is why Compaq has strong partnerships with more than 20 leading software vendors, to extend and enhance the basic Windows CE functionality so it can support enterprise applications. The following sampler shows some of the solutions Compaq uses to bring more enterprise features to the Compaq Aero 8000® than any other product in its class.

 

ENHANCED SECURITY AND SMART CARD APPLICATIONS

Security management features in the Compaq Aero 8000® are significantly more powerful than those found on competing products. Authentication procedures provide increased security when a corporate network is accessed, and Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption provides a safer environment for accessing Web-based applications. An additional built-in Smart Card reader enhances security even further.

 

BASIC SECURITY FEATURES
The following basic security features are provided with each unit of the Compaq Aero 8000® handheld computing device:
  • Integrated flash memory. This semi-permanent memory retains the user's most critical information (password, calendar, contacts, and appointments) even when the batteries are drained or removed. The Compaq Aero 8000® can be set for automatic internal backup to ensure that vital information is always updated and safe.
  • Non-erasable, encrypted password. Compaq's unique password enhancements allow very tight control over access to the Compaq Aero 8000®. A non-erasable, encrypted password is stored in the integrated flash memory. This protects the password even when the batteries are removed.
  • Password reminder. The Compaq Aero 8000® uses a one-of-a-kind password reminder system. If a password is forgotton, a special "reminder" feature provides hints regarding a special, second password.
  • Password delay. A password delay feature allows users to deactivate passwords for up to eight hours. This feature is helpful when users anticipate frequently turning their units off and on, and they do not want to re-enter their passwords each time.
  • Backup/restore to flash PC cards. The "backup and restore to flash memory" feature copies all the data from the Compaq Aero 8000® to removable PC flash cards. This unique feature allows users to back up data while away from their desks.

SMART CARD CAPABILITIES
The Compaq Aero 8000® is the first H/PC Pro platform to take full advantage of the latest Smart Card technology, long used in Europe for

financial applications, but now being used increasingly for a wide range of authentication and data storage options in computing environments worldwide. Each Compaq H/PC Pro unit comes equipped with a built-in Smart Card reader in addition to the more standard Microsoft Windows password control features.

To prevent data theft or unauthorized use, administrators can set up the units so that they do not start up without a specific Smart Card insertion, a password, or — for maximum security — both. Alternately, administrators can disable these features if stringent security is not required.

Importantly, the Smart Card reader is also a writing device, so IM can tailor the contents of the Smart Card to fit specific applications, instead of changing the application to fit the Smart Card. The writing feature can be used to load the Smart Card with special data sets, digital signatures, or other information that can be used for login or interface with applications. Compaq is enhancing the functionality and benefits of its Smart Card components by partnering with Schlumberger Ltd., a company world renowned for its long history of innovation in the Smart Card applications field.

 

SMART CARD SOLUTIONS
In addition, a partnership with Aliroo Ltd. is bringing two important applications to the H/PC Pro platform:
  • PrivaSUITE™ allows companies to have secure e-mail, fax, or file encryption for their mobile workers while on the road. PrivaSUITE can encrypt any type of document, in any application, for complete correspondence and document privacy. Built-in key management and electronic document shredding are just two of the extra convenient utilities found in this package. When used with Smart Cards, keybook information on the Smart Card can be accessed by the PrivaSUITE key management utility. Access to the Smart Card, including keybook data, is electronically protected by a private identification number (PIN).
  • PrivaSEAL™ provides digital signature capability so that Compaq customers can verify the authenticity of encrypted transactions. Users can sign and authenticate documents to make sure that they came from the right person. Signatures are embedded in documents through a simple drag-and-drop procedure, and validation of the signature is accomplished through a simple double-click. Authenticated signatures can be applied to a wide variety of file tyeps and applications, including most Microsoft, Lotus, and Corel files, at the document level or even at the paragraph level.

Since Compaq is the first company to apply Smart Card, encryption, and digital signature technologies to the H/PC Pro platform, it is likely to become the standard platform for a range of advanced solutions. In Europe, for instance, the Compaq Aero® 8000 is already slated for use in healthcare applications that require encryption and authentication. Others are likely to follow.

 

TERMINAL EMULATION
Seventy percent of all corporate data still resides on mainframe computers within large-scale enterprises and most of it is mission-critical. For this reason, there is still a great need within the

user community for access to this crucial source of business intelligence. With mainframes, one of the most common access methods is terminal emulation, the ability to run mainframe data access screens on ordinary PCs.

FutureSoft brings terminal emulation to the H/PC Pro platform. Loading this client software into the device — with proper connectivity installed — gives immediate access to the mainframe, just as though the user were sitting at a mainframe console. Mainframe access can be accomplished through a standard network connection, a Proxim wireless LAN, or even through a dial-up connection using a wired or wireless modem. The FutureSoft DynaComm product supports a wide variety of terminal emulations including ADDS VP/60, ANSI, SCO-ANSI, AT&T 605/705, TeleVideo 925/950, Wyse 50/60, and the VT Series (52/100/102/220/320/420), plus telnet, TAPI, and FTP.

 

MOBILE THIN CLIENT SOLUTIONS
Some users may not be satisfied with the scaled-down versions of office software found on most Windows CE devices. Access to full Windows applications can be accomplished

through a variety of technologies, including Citrix thin client solutions and the Microsoft Terminal Server. In this case, the full application runs on a remote server and the thin client software simply provides a screen display of the remote application as it runs — plus keyboard interactivity so the remote user can control the application.

Citrix software makes it possible to run thin client applications over very thin communications lines or using wireless modems. Unlike competing H/PC Pro devices, the Compaq Aero 8000® loads the thin client software directly into ROM for quick and easy access to existing Citrix WinFrame application servers housed within the enterprise.

No other PC company has the experience and expertise that Compaq has in the thin client market. The Compaq partnership with Citrix has been long and fruitful — over 50 percent of CITRIX applications are housed on Compaq servers. The Compaq thin client server group works with CITRIX very closely to ensure the best available performance and seamless integration.

 

SYNCHRONIZATION WITH LOTUS NOTES
Traditional implementations of Windows CE allow synchronization of data with standard Microsoft Office applications, including Outlook, Word, and Excel.

Importantly, solutions options provided by Compaq alliance partners also allow synchronization with other types of applications, including Lotus Notes, which is used by a significant segment of the enterprise computing market.

All in Sync!™ software, provided by River Run Software Group, enables Compaq Aero 8000® H/PC Pro users to synchronize the Windows CE calendar, tasks, and contacts databases with the calendar and scheduling data stored in a corporate Lotus Notes server. RiverRun software also provides enhanced data synchronization with Microsoft Exchange servers that goes beyond the capabilities built into Windows CE. Users who need dual access to both Lotus and Exchange will have a double advantage thanks to this solution.

Notes integration provides full server synchronization of all contact and calendar information and e-mail. In this mode, the user client device synchronizes directly with the server, effectively bypassing the normal desktop synchronization process.

 

ACCESS TO LEGACY E-MAIL SYSTEMS
Corporate users of H/PC Pros often need access to other types of legacy e-mail systems, including Microsoft Mail, Microsoft Exchange Server, Lotus cc:Mail, and Novell GroupWise. River Run's Mail on the Run! product for Windows CE enables the Compaq Aero 8000® H/PC Pro to connect directly — or over telephone, circuit-switched cellular, or wireless networks — with the world's most popular mail systems. It is the ideal product for remote scheduling over wire-line, wireless, and packet data networks and, as with all remote access products, it enables users to work any place, at any time.

Mail on the Run! provides automatic connection to the LAN, and seamlessly integrates mail retrieval with the standard Windows CE Inbox application, allowing users to send, receive, store, forward, and reply to messages. Users can process e-mail online, download copies of all mail, or download headers only and mark them to be copied, moved, or deleted the next time a connection is made.

The product includes two components:

  • client software installed on the Compaq Aero 8000®
  • agent software that processes requests on behalf of all the clients, and communicates with client devices over wireless as well as wire-line networks

The agent software runs on either a Windows 95 computer or through Remote Access Services (RAS) on the Windows NT platform. The agent can support access by multiple concurrent users who need access to the mail server. When users connect, the agent confirms the user name and password before proceeding. After access has been approved, the agent processes requests. When the transactions are finished, the handheld device automatically logs off and disconnects.

The software's highly efficient e-mail processing maximizes the productive use of employee time and expensive wireless connection time. Mail on the Run! provides sophisticated support for file attachments, automatically converting files into native file formats, and letting users set guidelines for whether or not to download attachments. It supports remote access to the mail system's address books on the LAN, the ability to filter messages based on various criteria, and the ability to transparently encrypt messages using a variant of industry-standard DES encryption optimized for the H/PC Pro to ensure secure communication. A special Mail Alert! option allows users to be paged when certain messages are received in the mailbox, pushing the first few lines of critical e-mail to the user's alphanumeric pager.

 

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE/DATABASE ACCESS

The mobile user now has various options for connectivity to back-office database systems carrying mission-critical information. Companies with existing database systems such as Oracle or Sybase that want to push data out to mobile workers will find software that runs on the CE platform to provide data connectivity:

 

ORACLE LITE
Designed especially for the mobile computing market, Oracle Lite can be used to deliver existing Oracle database applications to mobile workers via the H/PC Pro.

Typically, this is done by creating a forms-based application for Windows CE and synchronizing the data with an existing Oracle database. Using Oracle Lite, enterprise managers can provide database functionality for mobile workers who need to collect or update corporate data while in the field. As an example:

  • Field workers can use Oracle Lite to order new parts and update customer service records from remote locations.
  • Sales reps can have access to customer data, competitive information, and inventory pricing and availability, as well as sales and marketing reports.
  • Branch office workers can use the product in stand-alone mode to view and update database tables, synchronizing as needed to a central database.

The software has a small footprint (less than 750 KB) and allows applications to replicate data through standard network connections, or through low-bandwidth dial-up or wireless connections. Replication over the Internet or intranet is handled using HTTP in conjunction with the Oracle Application Server. Oracle Mobile Agents (OMA) are used to optimize the process when replicating over wireless or other low-bandwidth connections. Data updates can also be stored in files in flash memory and synchronized later, when conditions are optimal.

Oracle Lite supports Jave stored procedures and triggers, and is compatible with ODBC, JDBC, embedded SQL, and OKAPI. It provides bidirectional replication to enterprise servers and allows flexible application development using standard programming tools (C, C++, VB, and Java).

 

SYBASE ADAPTIVE SERVER ANYWHERE
Sybase, Inc. provides a technology called Adaptive Server Anywhere for computers such as the Compaq Aero 8000® H/PC Pro.

Adaptive Server Anywhere is a completely functional database system with a small footprint that allows mobile users to:

  • Compile and update data offline and store it on the handheld computer for later synchronization with network database servers.
  • Access and update corporate data from any location with transparent bi-directional replication of data to host servers using SQL Remote technology.
  • Administer databases through connectivity to Windows desktop tools, such as Sybase Central and SQL Modeler.
  • Develop new applications on the CE platform that are compliant with ODBC, ANSI-standard stored procedures and triggers, and other advanced development models.

A key value of using the Compaq Aero 8000® as a database platform is that it makes sales reps and field-service reps less reliant on the desktop computer as their sole data source, moving the action closer to the customer, where service can be provided more quickly and easily. Creating a mobile data solution also saves costs by allowing database administration to be more effectively centralized and efficiently replicated.

Sybase offers an UltraLite deployment option for Adaptive Server Anywhere that provides an application-optimized, ultra-small database for use on palm-size PCs and embedded devices, which can be used in tandem with handheld PCs and desktop computers for specialized applications. Adaptive Server Anywhere is available as a component of Sybase's SQL Anywhere Studio.

 

CLIENT-SERVER INTEGRATION
@hand provides a convenient, sophisticated way to connect customized Windows CE applications to back-office and front-office systems, including SQL Server, Oracle, AS/400, and DB2 databases, plus a host of third-party enterprise applications including Siebel, Vantive, Aurum, PeopleSoft, Baan, Remedy, Trilogy, Calico, Platinum, Oracle, and Sybase. The @hand offering includes a proprietary client-server architecture that provides the following features and benefits:
  • Integration services. Object-based agents that integrate with third-party applications to allow exchange of objects and data. Includes a component management feature that is integrated with Microsoft Transaction Server and Message Queue.
  • Foundation services. This level of architecture provides application management, database management, system management, and change management for handling the distribution and installation of applications, data abstraction, authentication, directory services, system performance monitoring, user/group management, and data synchronization.
  • Connectivity. Base-level services for handheld-to-server connectivity, including connection management (user authentication, compression, and encryption) and data transport (raw bi-directional file synchronization).

The @hand client provides a runtime environment with a rich set of services that support Visual Basic, C++, Java, and HTML. The application server runs in a Microsoft Windows NT environment with support for Microsoft SQL Server and Oracle. System management is integrated with the Microsoft Management Console and Systems Management Server (SMS). The solution supports handheld-to-server connectivity through a desktop docking station, but it stores data and application changes offline when the handheld unit is undocked or the server is unavailable. Using @hand development tools, companies can create their own applications that tie into back-end servers.

 

DATABASE INTEGRATION
Synchrologic is another solution for providing data synchronization and database replication in scalable mobile applications that support many different databases (Oracle, Microsoft SQL Server, IBM DB2,

SQL Anywhere, Sybase, Informix, and Microsoft Access). Such applications are used in a variety of endeavors, such as sales-force automation, field-service automation, home healthcare, and remote retail.

Companies that have already implemented field sales automation on a notebook computer may be interested in porting it to the Windows CE platform, or even creating new applications from scratch. For example, an insurance company might want to have its claim forms online so that agents could enter data, with many types of applications running in the background to synchronize with database servers on the back end. Hospital and healthcare organizations might develop specific applications to capture patient data as mobile nurses make their rounds.

 

MOBILE INTERNET APPLICATIONS
Intertop Corporation creates tools that allow companies to develop intelligent Internet applications that use the web browser (in this case, Pocket Internet

Explorer) as a front-end client that allows users to collect data, transmit it via wireless, or batch it and download it at the end of the day. Intertop CE Application Builder provides an integrated development environment for multi-tiered Internet applications running on Windows CE. Companies that use these tools can leverage their existing skills in Visual Basic development to quickly develop Internet applications for the mobile user. The product provides testing and debugging tools for client GUI, client logic, and server logic, and includes a Visual CE toolkit for client emulation.

Internet applications developed using these tools are typically used to help mobile workers access data stored on back-end servers. For instance, a salesperson might use web-based applications to retrieve pricing, inventory, or financial data, then update the data and resynchronize it with the host database. A field-service rep could get access to online parts manuals and maintenance procedures, then order parts as needed and complete work-order forms. Many of the hardcopy documents now lugged around by mobile professionals can be placed online in a web environment to provide instant access to reams of information without the heavy burden of paper. Naturally, web-based applications are most useful for transmitting data and published information across the Internet, extranets, and intranets.

 

WIRELESS ACCESS SOLUTIONS
Compaq supports a number of solutions that provide wireless access to users of the Compaq Aero 8000® within the continental U.S. Vendors supported include:
  • Research in Motion (interactive pagers and modems for BellSouth network)
  • Sierra Wireless (wireless modems for CDPD networks)
  • Proxim (wireless LAN cards)
  • InfoWave (server connectivity for Exchange)
  • GoAmerica (nationwide wireless Internet Service Provider)

Wireless connectivity is an enabling technology for most of the other solutions listed in this white paper and can be used for any application that requires Internet, extranet, intranet, or corporate LAN/WAN connectivity without the use of wires, including e-mail, mobile thin client computing, web applications, and others.

A wireless solution is ideal for users who do not have easy access to network cables or phone lines for dial-up modem connections into the network (RAS). Adopting a wireless solution involves purchasing the correct hardware, and in some cases, software, as well. For wireless access outside company premises, a wireless networking service may also be involved. For example, a company wanting to implement an Exchange application over wireless connections might use Compaq's bundled solution — a combination of InfoWave software and a Sierra Wireless modem operating over a CDPD network such as AT&T. Wireless Internet access is available through GoAmerica, a U.S.-based ISP that offers several Internet access plans, and even provides the wireless modem hardware that helps users connect to the Internet from any mobile location.

Other types of hardware components are available for different wireless needs. Proxim's Wireless LAN hardware includes not only a wireless networking card installed in the unit's PCMCIA slot, but other wireless networking components, including special routers and hubs that serve as the backbone of the wireless network.

Some wireless solutions available in the U.S. are not available for international markets. Proxim wireless networks will work anywhere they are installed, but other wireless services mentioned here are not available outside the U.S. Compaq is currently working to locate and partner with European vendors of wireless networking equipment and services that support GSM (the Global Standard for Mobile Communications).
