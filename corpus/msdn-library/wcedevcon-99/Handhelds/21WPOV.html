The Compaq Aero® 2100:
The Palm-Size PC with the Advanced Color Screen

Handheld Products Group Marketing, Displays & Peripherals Division

Compaq Computer Corporation

Since its humble beginnings as a personal information manager (PIM) and electronic organizer, the palm-size PC has grown to be an essential part of the technological arsenal of most fast-paced, mobile business professionals. In the process, few could have predicted just how useful these tiny devices would become. Now much more than just a contact management tool, the palm-size PC is becoming a platform for advanced personal productivity as well as enterprise-level solutions.

To answer the growing demand for this type of high-end functionality, Compaq produced the Compaq Aero® 2100 — a palm-size device that contains some of the most advanced technologies and features available on the market today. This white paper explores the evolution of the palm-size PC and explains in detail the technologies that are helping the Compaq Aero 2100 redefine this increasingly important market segment.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Today's fast-paced business world calls for computing solutions that are increasingly trim, mobile, economical, and effective. Professional business users are rarely found sitting at their desks anymore. Instead, they are more likely to be found moving rapidly between conference rooms, branch offices, and airline terminals, trying frantically to stay in touch with a rapidly changing environment. The tools they use include cell phones, modems, and pagers — plus, increasingly, small mobile information management appliances such as handheld or palm-size computers.

Until now, the main application for these mobile computing devices has been embodied by the personal information manager (PIM), a type of software that provides a few standard options such as contact management, to-do lists, and appointment scheduling. But as the demand has grown for mobile computers, so have the user's need for advanced equipment that can handle the changing dynamics of an increasingly demanding business environment. Today's mobile professional must juggle not just meeting schedules and contacts, but a heavy load of e-mail, competitive data, financials, and other information delivered through global networks.

In designing the Compaq Aero 2100, Compaq saw the need for a product that would pull together all these trends and take the palm-size PC to the next level. The result is a device that combines the functions of a PIM, audio center, e-mail client, and fax machine (with modem or cell phone) — all into a single product. The Compaq Aero 2100 includes some of the most advanced features available today, including a superior color display with one of the largest TFT screen sizes available; reflective technology for easy visibility under extreme lighting conditions; advanced lithium-ion battery technology; ergonomic design for efficient single-hand operation; easy connectivity for fax, modem and network applications; and a bundle of advanced productivity software including Microsoft Windows CE.
