EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Compaq C-Series is the first professional handheld PC. It is designed specifically to address the needs of mobile professionals who need a highly mobile productivity and information access tool with essential functionality while away from the desktop PC.
C-Series Solutions — Remote Access (RAS), E-Mail, Internet access and more

The C-Series is a feature-rich handheld PC that provides a comprehensive set of productivity and communication features in a highly mobile design. With an integrated 33.6 KBPS software modem, it is very well suited for remote access to the company network or office/home PC, and for checking e-mail or browsing the web while on the road. The software also allows users to prepare letters and spreadsheets while away from their offices, and synchronize data with their notebook or desktop PCs.

Integrating the C-Series into the Company LAN or Corporate IS Network

With its setup utilities, ROM-based Windows CE Operating System, Pocket office applications, communications capabilities, and unique security and asset control features, the C-Series is well suited to be integrated as a "mobile client" into an existing company/corporate network.
