Advanced Color Display

The Compaq Aero 2100 solves readability problems by using a high resolution active-matrix (TFT) display that provides exceptionally crisp color images on a larger screen format than can be found on most competing models.

  • Color TFT Active Matrix Screen provides a high-resolution display that is comparable to the advanced screens found on notebook PCs and flat panel displays. The thin-film transistor (TFT) liquid crystal display (LCD) provides a separate transistor for each pixel in the display, which results in absolutely bright, crisp flicker-free images. Reading from a TFT screen reduces eyestrain and is much easier than the passive matrix screens found on most competitor products. It provides distortion-free viewing over a wide range of viewing angles with no ghosting, flicker, or loss of color fidelity.

  • Reflective technology provides unparalleled screen visibility in the kind of bright-light environments — including outdoor sunlight — that tend to wash out the displays of back-lit products, making them extremely hard to read. Compaq's unique reflective technology enhances visibility by providing front lighting and also by reflecting ambient light off the back surface of the display, making the unit as easy to read outdoors as it is indoors. Back-light screens are not powerful enough to create the contrast required for easy viewing in bright light environments; they are analogous to a flashlight used in sunlight.
  • Three illumination settings allow the user to optimize viewing in any lighting condition:
    • ambient lighting with no illumination (default) still benefits from the improved visibility provided by reflective technology;
    • low brightness setting provides slightly enhanced visibility under moderately difficult lighting conditions; and
    • high brightness provides the highest visibility in extremely dark environments.
  • 256-color palette provides a much richer presentation of graphic images that is possible on 2-16 color grayscale palette (monochrome) screens of the leading Palm-size PCs. Color enhances the users ability to distinguish between items on the screen, and helps highlight important action items and tasks. The color screen also replicates the familiar environment of the user's desktop PC, thus reducing learning time and confusion.
  • Finer pixel pitch creates a much sharper image than found on most screens of this type.
  • High-resolution screen greatly enhances the amount of viewable information. The 240x320 resolution on Compaq Aero 2100 is three times greater than the PalmPilot's 240x320 resolution (76,800 pixels versus 25,600 pixels). This dramatically improves the amount of viewable screen real estate, allowing users to see more at a glance, as dramatized in the following set of photos.

Advanced Battery Technology

The Compaq Aero 2100 is among the first to bring powerful lithium-ion battery technology to palm-size PCs, providing long battery life and rechargeable convenience to match the fast-paced lifestyle of today's mobile professionals. Advanced battery technology also helps support the advanced power needs of the TFT color display and other advanced features in the Compaq Aero 2100.

Advanced Docking Cradle (recharge/sync)

  • Lithium-ion technology. Lithium-ion is the battery technology that has contributed heavily to the growing popularity and usefulness of high-end cell phones and notebook computers by providing long battery life and easy rechargeability. Lithium-ion offers more than double the charge-by-weight of comparable nickel metal hydride (NiMH) batteries and even more charge-by-weight than the standard lead acid batteries used in most palm-size devices.

  • Long life. Due to its advanced design, the Compaq Aero 2100's lithium-ion battery can provide the average user with up to two weeks of usage from a single charge depending on the type and frequency of daily usage. The operating life of 6-10 hours is double or triple the battery life offered by most of today's monochrome palm-size PCs, and it helps guarantee maximum productivity for the mobile professional by providing continuous service during even the busiest workdays.
  • Easy recharging. The Compaq Aero 2100 eliminates the need for additional hardware; plugging the device's AC adapter into a power socket automatically recharges the battery. The deluxe docking cradle with integrated battery charger allows users to automatically recharge their batteries at the same time as they are synchronizing their PCs.
  • Extended options. Other Compaq Aero 2100 power options include both a car adapter and additional battery packs for extended operability. Since the batteries are easily replaceable, heavy users can take along a second charged battery pack for times when they will be away from their PC for extensive periods of time.
  • Backup battery. Users can be confident that their data will be safe when replacing the main battery, because the Compaq Aero 2100 offers a coin-size lithium backup battery (CR2032) that automatically takes over when the main battery is removed for replacement.

 

Ergonomic Design

Design is one of the most challenging aspects of any palm-sized device, and one of the largest contributors to customer productivity and satisfaction. The challenge is to provide the most power and functionality possible in the smallest of spaces - essentially doing the most, with the least. In this sense, the Compaq Aero 2100 represents a breakthrough in user-friendly design:

  • One-handed operation. The dial on the side of the Compaq Aero 2100 enables one-handed operation — even while holding a briefcase, talking on the telephone, or taking notes. Users can scroll up or down and execute actions by pressing the dial in, just like the click of a mouse on a desktop PC. A special "instant exit" button just below the dial acts like an Escape key, allowing the user to quickly back-track (page up, end the current operation, or return to the previous window).

  • Secure integrated stylus holder locks the stylus into place to avoid accidental loss. Gently pressing the stylus automatically pops it free for easy removal. One pitfall of competing products is their lack of a secure holding mechanism, which often results in a lost stylus.
  • Touch sensitive display responds to pressure from the stylus or fingertip, allowing users to easily navigate through the Windows CE environment by touching buttons and other window controls.
  • Programmable application launch buttons also respond to either stylus or fingertip, allowing the user to instantly launch up to four separate applications without having to search through Windows menus. A small recess in each button accepts the stylus point so that the user does not have to put the stylus aside to press the program buttons.

  • Instant on/off and lighting toggle. A single on/off button turns the Compaq Aero 2100 on or off, allowing instant access to critical information in less than a second. Instant exit protects both the battery and data security. This button also doubles as a toggle switch to dim or intensify screen lighting.

  • Three-mode alarm provides flexibility in how Compaq Aero 2100 alerts the user to important reminders or appointments. Users may choose between a silent vibration (similar to a pager), an audible chirp, or a silent visible flash from the built-in light-emitting diode (LED). The vibration alarm may be appropriate either in loud areas, such as an airport or busy restaurant, or very quiet places such as in meetings or conferences where the user does not want to disrupt the proceedings. The audible beeper is ideal for office situations where the user is heavily engaged in an activity or is not physically wearing the device. The flashing LED is another unobtrusive alarm technique preferred by some users.

Ease-of-use features add convenience to the users daily activities and improve the speed at which users can react to business situations.

 

Advanced Audio and Entertainment Platform

The Compaq Aero 2100 functions as a portable voice recorder as well as an audio information and entertainment platform. Compaq included several robust hardware and software features, including Audible Content Player™, instant recording feature for voice notes, an integrated microphone, and an input/output jack.

  • One-touch voice recorder. No need to fumble with the stylus and scribble notes. Even when the Compaq Aero 2100 is turned off, the user can easily capture a phone number or take a customer order by simply pressing the single-touch "record" button on the top corner of the device and speaking into the integrated pinhole microphone. The record button instantly turns the Compaq Aero 2100 into a hand-held voice recorder, capturing audio notes that can be easily played back at any time through the built-in speaker or through headphones. Users can transfer and play back audio content with ease using simple screen controls. Voice notes are quicker and easier to take, and help eliminate the endless profusion of handwritten notes.

  • Support for microphones, headphones, or speakers. In addition to the built-in microphone, a convenient audio input/output jack allows users to plug in an external microphone for higher-fidelity recordings. Likewise, users can play back audio files aloud through the built-in speaker on the back of the unit or in privacy through plugged in headphones. The Compaq Aero 2100 even allows you to plug in speakers for higher fidelity or use special adapters to play back recorded content through your home or car stereo system.

  • Audible Content Player™ This proprietary software included with every Compaq Aero 2100 unit can be used to playback digital audio files downloaded from www.audible.com. Users may choose from over 4,000 titles, including:

    • business publications (Wall Street Journal, Harvard Business Review)
    • audio books with fiction or non-fiction titles (NYT Bestsellers)
    • radio programs (NPR's Marketplace, lectures, plays), and more.

 

Expandability, Connectivity, and Wireless Features

As palm-sized PCs become more prevalent in the business market, there is a virtually limitless need for enhanced functionality to meet specific user requirements. The Compaq Aero 2100 provides the solution through an industry-standard CompactFlash expansion slot and an infrared port for rapid connectivity to other devices.

CompactFlash Expansion Cards

CompactFlash Type 2 expansion cards dramatically improve the versatility of the palm-size PC, by providing many different types of extended functionality, including:

  • Backup memory (20-40 MB)

  • Fax/modem support (up to 56 Kbps)
  • Network connectivity (LAN/WAN/Internet)
  • I/O cards (serial and parallel port)
  • Bar code scanners

Wireless CF cards will be released in increasing numbers for increasingly sophisticated applications throughout 1999. The Compaq Aero 2100 is unique, however, in that it provides a specially shielded expansion slot that lets users employ advanced wireless communications without interfering with the unit's internal display or processor. On many competing products, emissions from wireless modems tend to disrupt the microprocessor and distort the display.

CompactFlash is electrically and functionally the equivalent of a PCMCIA card, but is only about one-third the size, making it the ideal expansion solution for palm-size and handheld PCs. Moreover, it adds little weight to the unit (1/3 ounce or 8 grams), is less expensive to produce, and has no impact on the size of the unit because it slides into the existing expansion slot. Compaq offers its own CF enhancements for modem and memory upgrade, with many more options available from third-party vendors.

Infrared Port

The Compaq Aero 2100 includes an industry-standard infrared port (IrDA) that provides wireless connectivity with other palm-size devices, handheld PCs, notebooks, desktop computers, and printers. Wireless infrared technology allows exchange of files and information instantly without the inconvenience of a physical cable. Data is transported on an infrared beam that can be read by any compatible receiving device.
