C-SERIES PROFESSIONAL OPTIONS

For the professional needing more than the standard accessories, Compaq offers the following set of options to complement the C-Series. You can learn more about these options and standard features online at: www.compaq.com/products/handhelds.

  • Flash ROM option for corporations that want to ensure a simple Windows CE OS field upgrade path in the future (details to follow).
  • Cradle for fast and simple connectivity to PC and power. Kensington lock compatible for secure lock-down also available. This option is invaluable to organizations concerned about the theft of handheld PCs that might be left overnight at the user's desktop.
  • 16-MB Memory Expansion Module increases storage space. This option is invaluable to mobile professionals storing thousands of contacts or needing to temporarily store large amounts of corporate data.
  • Stylus 3-pack provides the opportunity to keep spares in various locations.
  • Auto-Sync Cable for the convenience of having an additional serial cable. This can be used on the road for connecting to portables or for synchronizing to a second PC at the office or home.
  • 10-MB Flash RAM PC Card from SanDisk allows users the opportunity to increase storage or create secure back-ups by adding 10-MB ATA compatible flash RAM storage.
  • Portfolio Case offers opportunity to store C-Series in a fully functional portfolio.
  • Carrying Case provides fully-cushioned protection for the C-Series with room to store PC Card options, notes, business cards, personal identification, and much more.
  • Slipcase offers protection of the C-Series in a sleek design.
  • Auto Adapter provides quick access to power while in an automobile. This option is invaluable to professionals traveling by car from site to site. Using the Auto Adapter, battery life can be extended while connecting back to a corporate site, an Intranet, or to the Internet.
  • 110-240V switching AC adapter for different countries.
  • VGA Adapter PC card provides C-Series users the opportunity to show a Pocket PowerPoint presentation on a 640x480 or 800x600 VGA display.
  • External battery recharger to separately charge up to two NiMH rechargeable battery packs. The battery charger can also be used to provide DC power to the C-Series for extended use.
