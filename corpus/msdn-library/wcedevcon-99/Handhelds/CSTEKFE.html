SUMMARY OF C-SERIES
KEY DIFFERENTIATING FEATURES

  • High-performance MIPs-based RISC processor for 'instant' computing.

  • Fast, integrated software modem — 33.6 Kbps, for swift e-mail, remote PC synchronization and internet access at extremely low power consumption (available in many countries).
    • Low power consumption on the modem extends battery life significantly over hardware or PC Card modems.
  • Expandability.
    • EDO RAM, models expandable up to 32 MB.
      • RAM capacities for thousands of contacts, years of calendar entries, or large presentation files.
      • High-performance 32-bit design to increase system performance and storage capability.
    • Industry-standard Type II PC card slot for memory and communication cards.
    • 16-MB of ROM (Read Only Memory), containing all available components of Windows CE — including Pocket PowerPoint and VB run-time — on a single, user-replaceable module.
  • Optimized for long battery life.
    • Internal recharging of batteries (NiMH rechargeable batteries are standard on color models) eliminates the need for additional hardware, simply plug in power and the NiMH rechargeable batteries are automatically charged.
    • User-selectable backlight brightness.
  • AC Adapter bundled with all models.
    • Quick Connect Serial Cable bundled with all models.
    • Simple setup for easy use and easy implementation into corporate IS network.
    • Compaq-only strong security and management features protect users and their information.
    • Pocket asset management allows tracking of mobile devices and each one's information.
  • Setup and synchronization utilities.
    • ISP Setup copies Internet settings from desktop PC to the C-Series for easy configuration.
    • Custom dictionary synchronizer copies spell check words added to the user's personal dictionary on the desktop PC down to the C-Series (and vice versa).
    • Favorites synchronizer keeps favorite Internet URLs on the C-Series in sync with those on the desktop PC (compatible with Netscape and Internet Explorer).
  • Bundle of software included to further meet a user's changing and expanding needs (varies by country and may change without notice).
    • River Run's Mail on the Run! extends compatibility to more e-mail systems, All In Sync! To popular group calendaring and scheduling systems, and Corporate Connection to corporate databases.
    • Bsquare's Bfax Express adds faxing capability.
    • CIC's QuickNotes allows the user on the go to quickly scribble notes in an intuitive and easy program.
    • CIC's SpellCheck for all Windows CE applications.
    • Odyssey's BizCalc offers fully functional financial calculator.
    • Pocket Carbon Copy (Compaq Exclusive).
