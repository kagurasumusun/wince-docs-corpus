CORPORATE SOLUTIONS PRESENTATIONS

Until now, professionals wishing to give a presentation had to use transparencies or lug a heavy portable to connect to a projection system. With the C-Series and the optional Voyager VGA out PC card, presentations can be made using the Pocket PowerPoint, integrated into the system ROM. The Voyager VGA out PC card supports user selectable resolutions of either 640 x 480 or 800 x 600. The flexible software permits presenters to mirror what is displayed on the C-Series to a projection system, such as the versatile, and lightweight In Focus LP™420.

The In Focus LP™420 weighs just 6.8 pounds, and provides 500 lumens of brightness at a true SVGA (800 x 600) resolution. The combination of the C-Series and the In Focus LP™420 gives business presenters a solution that is truly portable.
