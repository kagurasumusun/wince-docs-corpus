VALUE -ADDED SOLUTIONS

Compaq Computer Corporation

The bottom line in mobile computing is simply value. The most successful products will be those that provide the most consistent value in terms of business solutions that solve specific, tangible problems. A few examples of value-added solutions are listed below.

Mobile Assistant

The most basic value-add is the one that provides everyday benefits to the greatest number of people. In this sense, it helps to take a look at how the Compaq Aero 2100 can help the mobile professional cope with a plethora of needs that arise during an ordinary business day. Use of the Compaq Aero 2100 can provide hours of extra productivity per day:

  • Reviewing documents or answering email during idle time while travelling;

  • Sending and receiving e-mail from the hotel room;
  • Taking notes or action items unobtrusively in meetings;
  • Checking the agenda of a busy day to avoid conflicts or delays;
  • Setting appointments or looking up contact information while away from the desk;
  • Present a sales or product presentation at the clients office;
  • Responding to a customer request with a well-calculated offering, on the spot; and
  • Checking stock levels immediately — using dial-up networking or wireless technologies — and being able to make the best offer to the customer immediately.

The time saved in all of these activities can result in serious productivity gains that quickly pay for the cost of the device as well as boost corporate profitability and return on investment.

 

Barcode Data Entry

In scenarios where a more specific application is required and tied to a dedicated task (mobile data collection and data entry, forms based applications etc.) the productivity gains can be significantly higher. Mobile solutions contribute to productivity by eliminating steps in the data entry process and by reducing the cycle times needed.

In the case of data entry, it is possible to use a barcode scanner attached to the Compaq Aero 2100 using a CompactFlash expansion card and accomplish — with equipment valued at $700 — the same tasks as more expensive scanning equipment that ranges from $3,000 to $4,000. Independent vendors are now working on an extensive range of solutions that will make it possible to collect bar code data automatically and process it on the palm-size PC, or transfer it to the desktop or other networked devices for more extensive analysis.

 

The Automated Salesforce

Advanced Windows CE devices like the Compaq Aero 2100 will put advanced salesforce automation applications into the hands of mobile sales agents throughout the world. Consider, for example, the case of a typical real estate agent who spends a great deal of time traveling through a city or territorial region, showing properties to prospective customers. With the advanced color display of the Compaq Aero 2100 for the first time it will be possible for the sales agent to carry around color photos as well as data on each of the properties in the company's listings.

With a few clicks of the stylus, it will also be possible to compute taxes and insurance on the property, as well as mortgage rates and payment schedules — all within the same device. If such applications can be used to display pictures and perform calculations on something as large as a home or office building, imagine how they could be applied to a diverse range of products such as automobiles, hospital supplies, carpet samples, art, and more.

 

Other Special Applications

There are many special applications for suitably equipped palm-size PCs currently under development by third-party vendors. In the near future, there will be versions of Oracle Light running on Windows CE devices providing direct access to database servers. A similar Sybase application is also under development, as are voice-recognition applications and other productivity applications for the mobile professional. Compaq is committed to developing partnerships with industry solution providers and assuring the quality of tested solutions on the palm-size PC platform for the benefit of its mobile professional customers.
