Display
256 Color high reflective Thin Film Transistor (TFT) liquid crystal display. TFT technology is displayed technology found in higher-end portable computers because of the crisp, sharp image it displays. The brilliant color enhances readability and improves the overall usefulness of the Palm-size PC. Reflective technology allows the user to clearly read the display in any environment — even in bright sunlight conditions that wash out the other Palm-size PC displays and make them unreadable. The display supports 320x240 resolution, three times that of the 3Com PalmPilot.
Power Supply
900mah Lithium-Ion battery provides up to 10 hours of use. Compaq is a market leader in bringing Lithium-Ion batteries to Palm-size PCs, a technology usually found in higher-end portable computers. When the Compaq Aero 2100 is placed in the docking station to synchronize, the Lithium-Ion rechargeable batteries are automatically charged. Connecting the AC Adapter also recharges the batteries.
Audio Capabilities
The Palm-size PC that also functions as a portable audio information and entertainment device. The robust audio capabilities enable users to record, transfer, and play back informative and entertaining audio content. Audible Splash™ software allows the Compaq Aero 2100 to play Audible audio files downloaded onto a PC from www.audible.com and transferred to the Palm-size PC upon synchronization. The instant record button allows convenient and quick voice notations at the press of a button. Listening to content is accomplished through an integrated speaker on the back of the unit, or headphones may be placed into the jack (adapter required) for private listening.
Ergonomic Design Features
Dial on the side of the Compaq Aero 2100 enables users to scroll up or down and execute actions with a single hand.
Integrated holding mechanism holds the ergonomic stylus securely in place.
Three-mode alarm (silent LED, audible chirp, or vibrate) provides flexibility in how the Compaq 2100 alerts the user.
Three settings include ambient lighting (no illumination) and low and high illumination settings.
Convenient front panel design includes five buttons, four for application launch keys and one as an on/off front light button.

Operating System
Microsoft Windows CE for the color Palm-size PC. Applications include character recognition, soft (digital keyboard), voice recorder, e-mail in box, task pad, contacts, calendar, calculator, Active Desktop (customizeable desktop), note pad, active sync (to synchronize with information on PC), mobile channels (for downloading specific web content), and back-end functionality to connect to remote computers.
