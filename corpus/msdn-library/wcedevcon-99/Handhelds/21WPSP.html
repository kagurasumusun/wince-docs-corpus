Mobile professionals need instant access to information anywhere, anytime, while they are away from their PC or company network. They are either self-employed, or work in a variety of enterprises ranging from small- and medium-sized businesses to large corporations. Many mobile professionals typically work in vertical markets such as health care, finance, insurance, sales, utility and other industries.

Understanding Market Needs and Product Categories

Each user group has its own unique needs. Though office workers are still heavy users of laptop and notebook computers, most mobile professionals also find that they need a small, lightweight, low-cost unit that combines ease-of-use, long-life, and innovative design with easy connectivity to email and other business communications systems such as fax. The needs of this group are best met using handheld and palm-size computers. Key differences between these mobile computing devices are shown in the chart below.

Mobile professionals who stand to gain the most from a palm-size device are PC users who need an extremely lightweight solution that allows single-handed operation in a busy multitasking environment. This type of user does not do a lot of keyboard work on the road, and requires something smaller and more easily portable than even the smallest handheld device. For maximum productivity, the palm-size PC must be easily synchronized with contact lists, schedules, e-mail in-boxes and other applications on the laptop, notebook, desktop, or corporate network.

Personal information management and e-mail are the most common uses for palm-sized PCs. But they are also finding a growing role in personal productivity applications, healthcare, sales force automation, and field service automation projects. Some applications require mobile data collection or remote access to server databases. The special vertical applications often needed for these markets can be ported relatively easily to the Windows CE platform since it uses the same developer tools as Windows 95/98/NT.

Compaq continues to conduct ongoing market research to understand and meet these growing customer needs, and is committed to becoming a leader in the palm-size PC market. The same skills and resources that have helped Compaq expand the Deskpro, server, and solutions market, will be brought to bear on providing the optimum palm-size PC solutions.
