Check your system volume settings

1 Double-click the Volume icon in the notification area of the taskbar.

If this icon does not appear, click the Start button, click Run, type sndvol32.exe, and then click OK.

2 In the Volume Control area, make sure that the Mute all check box is cleared.

3 Drag the volume control slider up.
