You might have a bad audio driver

Attempt to play other media files that include audio. If the audio content of other media files plays fine, the audio driver is working properly. If you suspect that you have a bad audio driver, install a new audio driver:

1 Find out who manufactured your audio card.

2 Connect to the Web site of the manufacturer of your audio card and download a new audio driver, or contact the manufacturer for an update.
