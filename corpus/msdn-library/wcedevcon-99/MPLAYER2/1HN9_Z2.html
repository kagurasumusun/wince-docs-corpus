Introducing Microsoft Windows Media Player

Microsoft Windows Media Player is a universal media player you can use to receive audio, video, and mixed-media files in most popular formats.

Use Media Player to listen to or view live news updates or broadcasts of your favorite sports team, to review a music video on a Web site, to "attend" a concert or seminar, or to preview clips from a new movie.

Take a look at the features that make Media Player the most comprehensive, simple, and efficient multimedia player:

Simplified playing of a wide range of file types

Easily tailored to meet your preferences

High-quality multimedia experience

Quick access to media content
