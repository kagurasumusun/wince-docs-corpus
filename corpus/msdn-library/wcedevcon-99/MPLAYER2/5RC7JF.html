5RC7JF

Specifies the number of packets of data lost during transmittal of the current media file from the source to your computer.

The media file is transmitted to your computer in small pieces, called packets. If a packet is not received, Media Player requests the packet multiple times from the source, until the packet is recovered or until Media Player runs out of time to retrieve it. A packet that cannot be recovered is recorded as lost. A lost packet may cause a skip in your video or audio playback.
