Specifies the view that will be used. A view is an arrangement of the components that appear in the player window.

In addition to the video area, the Standard view includes the following components of the Media Player: controls, Seek bar, Go To bar, status bar, and description area. You can customize the Minimal and Compact views by clicking the Custom Views tab. Click Standard to put the player in Standard view. In Standard view, all components of the player are displayed, except for closed captioning.
