27C.PA5

The player could be buffering

1 On the View menu, make sure that Standard is selected.

2 Check the status in the status bar at the bottom of the player window.

3 If the status is "Buffering," the player is storing part of the media file in memory. Wait until buffering completes; the file will then begin playing.

Note

· Typically, when Media Player opens a media file, it buffers for a short time and then begins playing the file. During playback, Media Player buffers other parts of the media file, to be played later. Buffering prevents skips and pauses in the playback of media files.
