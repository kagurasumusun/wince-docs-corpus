Hardware

Auto PC includes the following required hardware. Because each original equipment manufacturer (OEM) determines hardware design, device specifications vary.

  • A 60 MIPS or higher CPU
  • A faceplate and required keys
  • A color screen that is 256 x 64 x 3 pixels
  • 8 MB of ROM
  • 8 MB of RAM
  • A Compact Flash card slot
  • A CD ROM drive for data and music
  • A four or more channel preamplifier
  • A microphone
  • A serial port
  • A universal serial bus (USB) port
  • An infrared (IR) port
  • An AM/FM tuner
