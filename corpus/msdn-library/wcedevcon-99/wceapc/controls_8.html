Navigational Arrows Control

A navigational arrows control indicates when UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT, or ENTER keys are enabled. A navigational arrows control is placed on the right side of a form for consistency with other Auto PC applications. You can program the control using the IASUserNavArrows interface. However, because the control is used on many forms, the get_NavArrows method is also included in the IASForm interface.
