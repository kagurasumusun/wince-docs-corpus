Title: Extending Your Reach with Windows CE
Speaker: Paul Maritz

Abstract:
The incredible flexibility of Windows CE is enabling a revolution in information computing in all dimensions. Join Group Vice President, Paul Maritz, as he articulates how this dynamo operating system facilitates ubiquitous computing and connectivity through new form factors and user scenarios that were not previously possible. This kickoff keynote will set the tone for the rest of the conference by investigating existing uses of the OS, new announcements of form-factor and software advancements as well as industry partnerships that will fuel overall industry development.

Speaker Bio:
As Group Vice President of Applications and Platforms, Paul Maritz oversees the divisions contributing to Windows, Internet system software, consumer productivity software, tools, database and communications products, and evangelism activities. The divisions are: Desktop & Business Systems, Internet Platforms and Tools, Consumer Platforms, and the Softimage subsidiary, Desktop Applications Division (DAD) which includes the popular Office Suite and FrontPage. Maritz is also a member of Microsoft's Executive Committee. This group shares responsibility with Bill Gates, Microsoft's chairman and Chief Executive Officer, for broad strategic and business planning for the entire company. Since joining Microsoft in 1986, Maritz has managed various systems software product groups, including Microsoft's Networking and Windows units. Prior to joining Microsoft, Maritz spent five years at Intel Corporation. He has also worked for Burroughs Corporation and at the University of St. Andrews in Scotland. Maritz is a graduate of the University of Cape Town and of the University of Natal, South Africa. He studied computer science and mathematics.

Windows CE 2001: A Look in the Crystal Ball at the Future of Windows CE

Speaker: Harel Kodesh
As Vice President, Productivity Appliance Division at Microsoft Corporation, Mr. Kodesh is responsible for the specification, engineering and marketing of the Windows CE operating system and information appliances in the mobile and portable arenas that are based on Windows CE. Prior to his current position Harel pioneered the effort of developing small consumer appliances. Before that he managed Microsoft's Object Linking and Embedding group that created the OLE technology and programming model. Before joining Microsoft Harel was in charge of development of advanced wireless data technology devices for Motorola. He also held numerous technical and management positions in high tech start-up companies. Kodesh holds a master's degrees in electrical engineering business administration from Technion-Israel Institute of Technology and Tel Aviv University, Israel.
