THE Must-Attend Pervasive Computing Conference of the Year!

Here's your invitation to shape the way technology and people interact. With new form factors, major advances in integration, and the progress of networking systems, the Microsoft Windows CE operating system offers you the freedom to create the solutions you envision and to deliver them in any way you imagine. Any device can be intelligent. Everything can be connected.

The 1999 Windows CE Developers Conference (DevCon) promises to deliver what you asked for at last year's conference: more solid core technical content, fewer keynotes, and more face-time with the developers and architects. Get in-depth coverage of technologies in embedded systems, mobile computing and emerging technologies such as smart cards, integration with the television and telephone, eBooks, and home networking. Plus there will be breaking information about the many new offerings in the Windows CE family. Expect to be blown away by the release code-named "Cedar" which enables a whole new class of uses and features for embedded devices.

In order to provide you with specific coverage of the wide array of Windows CE technologies, we have organized this conference to allow more sessions and fewer keynotes. This lets you focus on the segments that are most relevant to your development efforts. In addition to detailed technical content, we will conduct several business development sessions aimed at lending insights into building these exciting new industries together.

Whether you build systems, write software, create add-on hardware, deliver content and services via the Internet - or if you want to develop your business with leading Windows CE technologies - this is the one and only Windows CE-exclusive conference to attend this year! See you June 6-9 at the Colorado Convention Center in Denver, Colorado!
