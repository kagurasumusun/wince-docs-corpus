  ACTIVITIES

The DevCon offers attendees a wide variety of activities designed to educate, entertain and provide time to network with their peers! Find out the details here.

Hands-On Area
Back by popular demand - this special area is where we’ll have the latest Windows CE-based devices on display. Talk to technical staff and see for yourself why Windows CE technology is the talk of the town.

Ask The Experts (ATE)
Always a highlight of our developer conferences, the ATE session allows you to get face-to-face with the developers and program managers who live and breathe Windows CE. Come prepared to ask any unanswered questions you may have from the conference sessions. This is one event you don’t want to miss!

Evening Events
Please join us for the Welcome Reception Sunday evening, June 6, on the trade show floor. Thoughout the conference we’ll have evening events for you to relax, talk to your peers, and, of course, fill up on food and beverages. Tuesday evening will feature headline entertainment:

An Evening With Sinbad!
Sinbad has built a loyal following with sold-out comedy shows and has gone from being one of America's best-loved and funniest comedian to national stardom on television and movies.
