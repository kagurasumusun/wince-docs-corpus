Product Position Statement (PPS)

Due to the growing portable computing market, peripheral manufacturers have been challenged to provide a standard expansion slot interface to make it easier for mobile users to add functionality to their portable systems. Out of this challenge has emerged the Personal Computer Memory Card International Association (PCMCIA). Since its inception, PCMCIA or PC Card has enabled the proliferation of portable peripherals by creating a standardized specification enabling increased peripheral compatibility, availability, and ease of use. Hence, PC Card peripherals such as data/fax modem, memory, networking cards and hard disk drives have enabled portable system users to obtain the same benefits from their portables as they do from their desktops.

One of the most technologically advanced PC Card peripherals available is the PC Card hard disk drive. Most older portable systems are equipped with 2.5" disk drives. Although 2.5" drives are small in size and light in weight, they are also traditionally limited in capacity.

Kingston Technology’s DataPak product family addresses the increasing storage needs for the portable-computing environment. DataPak is a Type III compatible PC Card hard disk drive with compact dimensions of .41" thick, 2.1" wide and 3.3" long (10.4mm x 53.3mm x 83.8mm). DataPak conforms to the PC Card standard and makes storage upgrades fast and easy. With its miniature design and large capacities (available in 520MB and 1GB uncompressed capacities), DataPak is small enough to fit into a shirt pocket and large enough to store one’s whole suite of applications and files. DataPak effectively allows for unlimited storage on PC Card Type III equipped portable systems as well as providing data transportability to other PC Card compatible systems and digital devices.

Kingston makes these many benefits affordable to the average consumer by combining high performance and quality. Kingston's DataPak is the most cost-effective PC Card hard disk drive available today.

Kingston Technology is a leading manufacturer of enhancement products for computers and digital devices. Kingston’s family of portable computing products includes memory, SSFDC, CompactFlash and DataFlash (flash memory storage). With the addition of DataPak products Kingston continues to expand its PC Card storage product line. As experts predict enormous growth in the PC Card market they’ll recognize Kingston as a leader in the industry.

Product Description:

DataPak is a removable PC Card Type III compliant hard disk drive available in two large storage capacities of 520MB and 1GB. The DataPak is specifically designed for increased storage capacity and portability for today’s popular portable computer systems and digital devices.

Popular Uses:

  1. Easily increases storage capacity
  2. Compressed up to 2GB for easy backup and storing of the primary fixed disk drive
  3. Sharing information among systems
  4. Fast and efficient data backup and recovery
  5. Ideal for accessing large amounts of information quickly (when using more than one DataPak)
  6. Removable for data security
  7. Hot swap capability for switching amongst other PC Card peripherals

Features and Benefits:

Feature: PC Card Type III compliant
Benefit: DataPak is fully compatible with most popular notebook personal computers and digital devices that have a PC Card Type III slot. Kingston has tested DataPak in some of the most popular portable systems available today, including Toshiba, Compaq and IBM notebook computers.
Feature: Up to one gigabyte (1040MB) of uncompressed and formatted data storage capacity
Benefit: DataPak 1GB is the highest "uncompressed" capacity PC Card compliant hard disk drive available today (also available in 520MB storage capacity). DataPak 1GB is compatible with disk compression utilities and can be compressed up to 2GB, if users choose to do so.
Feature: 128KB dual ported FIFO data with caching and look-ahead technology
Benefit: Up to 20MB transfer rate per second accessed at 12 ms seek time. For transfer rate, higher is better and for seek time, lower is faster. DataPak 1GB performance is one of the best in the industry.
Feature: Data backup
Benefit: Back up your data using standard DOS, OS/2, Windows 95, Windows 98, Windows NT or Macintosh O/S commands to protect against data loss.
Feature: Padded protective pouch
Benefit: Adds extra protection to DataPak during transport.
Feature: Rugged design to absorb high G-Shock
Benefit: DataPak can absorb G-shocks of 200G operating and 750G non-operating. This capability protects DataPak from failing under normal transporting and operating conditions. High durability of the DataPak allows for better protection of investment and lower failure rate than 2.5" hard disk drive.
Feature:  Multi-user system
Benefit: DataPak is ideal for corporations promoting the "pool sharing" technique for portable computer systems. Store all the application software on the shared system’s original hard disk drive and save the data files onto DataPak. Users can easily remove DataPak, hence, take their data with them, when they are finished using the system.
Feature: Optimum power management
Benefit: DataPak draws only 410 milli-amp of battery power during spin-up and only 10 milli-amp in both stand-by and sleep mode. This allows users to have longer battery life than other PC Card hard disk drives.
Feature: ‘Plug and play’ and dynamic loading compatible
Benefit: DataPak can be dynamically installed and removed from the system without powering down. To prevent unauthorized access and to protect investment, simply remove DataPak from the system and secure it in a safe place, or just put it in a shirt pocket and take it with you. DataPak effectively allows for unlimited data capacity.
Feature: One year warranty
Benefit: All DataPak hard disk drives come with a one year warranty. DataPak is also backed by Kingston’s free technical support and cross-shipping return policy.
