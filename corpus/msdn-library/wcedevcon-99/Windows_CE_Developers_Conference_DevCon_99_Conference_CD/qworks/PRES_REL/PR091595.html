Kingston Technology Announces
EtheRx PCI 10 Mbps Ethernet Adapters Under $100

Fountain Valley, CA— (September 15, 1995), Kingston Technology, the recognized leader in memory upgrade modules and fast-growing networking hardware manufacturer, today announced the immediate availability of EtheRx PCI Ethernet adapters. The adapters carry an attractive Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP), starting as low as $99.00, and support Windows 95.

EtheRx PCI adapters are available in two versions: a 10Base-T model with an RJ-45 interface and a 10Base-T and 10Base2 unit with both RJ-45 and BNC interfaces. Both PCI adapters feature QStart,   Kingston’s own graphical user interface configuration and diagnostics program. QStart picks up where PCI’s Plug and Play function leaves off.  QStart runs a four step diagnostics program on the adapter and then prompts the user to select from over 30 supported Network Operating Systems. After selection, installation may be completed in as few as three mouse clicks or keystrokes.

"At Kingston, we've dedicated ourselves to providing Ethernet hardware that has both great prices and easy installation," said Ron Seide, General Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit. "Our popular Workgroup Ethernet Hubs and EtheRx LC ISA Ethernet Adapters represent the first manifestations of this strategy. With EtheRx PCI, we’re simply furthering our strategy by supporting the increasingly popular PCI bus."

Based on the industry-leading Digital Semiconductor 21041 10Mbps PCI Ethernet controller, EtheRx PCI comes complete with a full suite of drivers: ODI for DOS, Novell Server Drivers, NDIS2 for DOS, NDIS2 for OS/2, NDIS3, Packet Drivers and SCO LLI drivers. Certification is pending for all popular Network Operating Systems including Novell NetWare and Microsoft Windows 95 and Windows NT.

Each EtheRx PCI is individually tested and is backed by a lifetime warranty. Staffed by Certified NetWare Engineers, technical support is comprehensive and provided free of charge. Additionally, technical support is toll-free in the United States and Canada. Available for immediate delivery, the EtheRx PCI 10Base-T version has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail price of $99.00 and the MSRP for the EtheRx PCI 10Base-T and 10Base2 interface adapter is $119.00.

EtheRx PCI is the latest in a line of low-cost, easy to install Ethernet networking hardware. The line includes eight and sixteen port Ethernet workgroup hubs as well as low-cost Ethernet ISA adapters. Kingston also provides a full line of PC Card (PCMCIA) networking hardware as well as Token Ring adapters.

With sales greater than $800 million in 1994, Kingston Technology is the recognized leader in memory, processor upgrades, networking and storage products for PCs, workstations and laser printers. In a recent Gallup poll, Kingston was recognized as the leading independent third party manufacturer of accelerator and memory products to the Fortune 1000. The firm markets its products through a world-wide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers.
