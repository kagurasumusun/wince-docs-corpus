Kingston Ships 48MB CompactFlash Memory Card
Setting New Low Price Per Megabyte

Extended CF Card Line Provides Reliable High-Speed Media Storage for Digital
Cameras and Portable Computing Devices

Fountain Valley, Calif. – December 10, 1998 – Kingston Technology Company today announced the availability of a 48MB CompactFlash (CF) card featuring the latest in high-density, ultra-performance NAND flash components. Kingston’s new, high-capacity CF card provides the lowest price per megabyte of reliable and high-speed media.

The new high-speed 48MB CompactFlash storage cards are particularly well-suited for applications such as megapixel digital cameras, which require more megabytes of flash memory storage for multiple images in the highest resolution mode. Kingston’s 48MB CompactFlash card increases the flexibility to seamlessly integrate data storage cards with computers, digital cameras, digital assistants, HPC, and palmtop personal computers.

"Kingston’s objective in developing a 48MB card is to serve vertically integrated markets and semiconductor companies in accelerating the market-wide adoption of the latest technologically innovative memory designs," said John Sutherland, director of new product development, Kingston. "We see opportunities where an end-user needs high capacity storage in this form factor, or where an OEM is planning to design a device around this form factor. Kingston can step in to provide the solutions necessary to ensure that the latest technology in memory is available in volume to the market as quickly as possible."

Kingston’s CF cards will also leverage advanced flash technology to significantly reduce product battery-dependence needed to retain data indefinitely. The 128Mbit NAND-type flash memory component will be the first device to be integrated in Kingston’s card for enhancing product performance by enabling easy file management through rapid write/erase capabilities.

"The announcement of our 48MB card demonstrates Kingston’s full support of the CompactFlash Association standards and all standards that propel growth in the memory design technology including miniature form factor NAND flash," said David Kwan, memory product manager, Kingston. "Kingston’s CompactFlash memory card can be easily removed from any number of host systems and slipped into a low-cost, high-transfer rate, PC Card reader/writer to move files instantaneously to a PC."

Kingston’s 48MB CompactFlash card, part number: (CF/48), has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $213.00, an industry-leading warranty and free technical support.
