Kingston Technology Increases Network Distribution Strength

Kingston Technology, the world's leading manufacturer of computer memory products, has announced that Micro-P, the UK distributor, will distribute its full range of PC and Apple networking products.

John Davis, the European Sales Manager for the Networking Business Unit, commenting on the appointment, explained: "As a focused distributor, Micro-P have a very effective sales and marketing strategy that helps maintain and increase the size of it's loyal customer base. Their values truly compliment ours and this will ensure both our and their continued growth."

Ron Seide, Kingston's General Manager of the Networking Business Unit added: "As our products and services continually evolved, it became increasingly apparent that there were opportunities for more than one distributor. We feel that Micro-P compliments the strengths that Datrontech PLC brings to our distribution strategy."

Mark Khar, Purchasing Director of Micro-P, commented: "We're delighted to see our strengths acknowledged in this way. Within the reseller market, we have established a strong reputation for supplying the best of breed products and excellent customer services. The Kingston networking product range is continually expanding and is absolutely ideal for our ever growing customer base."

Formed in 1980, Micro-P has established itself as one of the UK's leading distributors of monitors, printers and networking products. Based in both Accrington and Basingstoke, the company bases its philosophy on strong customer relations and knowing that doing the best for a small number of vendors is far better than doing an average job for many.

Kingston Technology is the recognized leader in manufacturing memory products in addition to providing quality processor, networking and storage upgrades for PCs, workstations, laptops/portables, servers and printers. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network for distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. Kingston is ISO 9001 certified as part of a continuing global effort to assure customers of the highest levels of both quality products and service.
