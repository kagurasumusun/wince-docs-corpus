New Low Price Point Kingston CF and ATA PC Card Readers Allow
High-Speed Transfer of Digital Photos and Data to Desktop, Networked PCs

Fountain Valley, Calif. – December 9, 1998 – Kingston Technology Company today announced the availability of a new line of PC Card Readers supporting Kingston’s line of CompactFlash and ATA flash memory storage cards. The new PC Card Readers are ideal for digital imaging or transferring data from a PDA or hand-held PC to a desktop PC quickly and effortlessly.

Kingston’s line of PC Card Readers provides an effective method bridging the gap between a user’s desktop and flash-compatible devices. "The Kingston PC Card Readers are designed to enable ease of data transfer to desktop systems," said David Kwan, memory product manager, Kingston. "Both of the ATA and CompactFlash PC Card Readers are portable and convenient to carry around for mobile photography and computing. Just plug your reader into your PC’s parallel port and you’re ready to go," added Kwan.

The Kingston ATA PC Card Reader, part number: (PCREAD/ATA) and the CompactFlash PC Card Reader, part number: (PCREAD/CF) both have a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price of $69.00.

Features of the PC Card Readers include:

  • Portable and convenient
  • Easy to install and use; simple plug and play
  • Remarkable high-speed image and data transfer to a PC or Macintosh
  • Windows CE, 95, 98, NT, and Macintosh OS compatible
  • Printer pass-through for simultaneous printer use
  • Hot swapping for replacing cards without restarting the computer
  • Power drawn for computer keyboard without needing an external power supply
