Kingston Technology Provides Ethernet Bundle
for Small / Home Offices at $174!

Fountain Valley, CA—(May 1, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing network manufacturer, today announced the release of the EtheRx SOHO (Small Office Home Office) Ethernet Hub and EtheRx Client PCI network adapter bundle. The SOHO hub/EtheRx Client PCI bundle is selling for as little as $174 Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail and features full support for Windows 95, Windows NT and Novell Network Operating Systems. The package is currently available through local resellers.

Shipping now in both a 5-port and 8-port configuration, the 5-port EtheRx SOHO hub and the 10BASE-T EtheRx Client PCI card bundle is sold for $174 MSRP while the 8-port EtheRx SOHO hub and the dual interface 10BASE-T and 10BASE2 EtheRx Client card bundle is priced at $204 MSRP. "By supplying an affordable alternative to ISA adapters we have made PCI a more attractive solution for the small office network," said Kevin Sherman, Marketing Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit.

Designed to provide years of worry-free operation, the EtheRx SOHO hub features the same reliability and investment in quality Kingston Technology has become known for in a smaller, ergonomically designed package. The EtheRx SOHO hub provides an inexpensive, easy to install solution for the small network user making twisted pair star topology networking a realistic alternative to less reliable and more expensive coaxial bus topology networks.

Based on industry proven controllers and backed by Kingston’s exceptional quality control, the EtheRx Client PCI network adapter brings PCI within the reach of the small network user. Packaged with QStart™, Kingston’s custom installation and diagnostics program, the PCI Client adapter provides fool- proof installation of ODI, NDIS 2 NDIS 3 Packet Drivers, LLI, MDI, and Macintosh Drivers which support all popular Network Operating Systems including Microsoft Windows 95 and NT, Microsoft Windows, Novell Netware, DEC Pathworks, Banyan VINES, SCO UNIX and Apple System 7.

Both the EtheRx SOHO Hub and the EtheRx Client PCI adapter are subject to the extensive Kingston 100% testing policy and are backed by a lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from our staff of Certified Netware Engineers. Technical support is toll free in both the US and Canada.
