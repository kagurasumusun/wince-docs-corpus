Kingston Networking Products Division
Shipping New TX Workgroup Hubs

Fountain Valley, CA -- (March 19, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, announced today the immediate availability of the first TX Workgroup Hubs designed specifically for the needs of the small to medium office. The new TX Workgroup hubs give growing businesses a cost-effective networking solution that allows a customer 100Mbps performance at a price point typically associated with 10Mbps solutions.

"Kingston’s new TX Workgroup hubs will allow users in a small office to share resources," said Chris Wong, Product Manager, Networking Products Division. "This will eliminate the need for dedicated peripherals at each workstation and users will be able to transfer large amounts of data at a high speeds," added Wong.

With a rugged, steel chassis design, the TX Workgroup hub is ideal for small to medium size businesses looking for increased networking speed. Other key features include:

· Universal power supply
  • Diagnostic LED display for easy troubleshooting
  • Cross over switch for straight-through cable wiring

Available immediately, Kingston’s TX Workgroup hubs are competitively-priced:

  • KNE4TX/WG four port TX Workgroup hub retails for $229.00
  • KNE8TX/WG eight port TX Workgroup hub is price at $389.00

All of Kingston’s TX Workgroup hubs undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a lifetime warranty. Technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the U.S. and Canada.
