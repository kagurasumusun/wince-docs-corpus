Kingston Expands Flash Product Line To Include
High-Capacity 112MB ATA Flash Cards

New 112MB Flash Card Sets New Low Price Point

Fountain Valley, Calif. – February 17, 1999 – Kingston Technology Company today announced the expansion of its Flash memory product line with the introduction of a high-capacity 112MB ATA Flash card. The new card sets a new low price point in the industry at $3.93/MB.

This new card allows digital camera users to take more high-resolution photos with a single card. Ideal for power users of the new Pro Edition of Windows CE products, the higher capacity of the ATA Flash card also means more memory for applications and data storage at more affordable pricing.

"Expansion of the Kingston Flash memory product line is another step toward offering the highest level of performance flash products. The 112MB ATA Flash card is the result of combining Kingston's expertise in memory design and innovative PC Card technology," said Chris Wong, product manager, Peripherals Division, Kingston. "Its low power consumption and virtual shock-resistant features, make it the ideal device for storing and transporting important digitized data information," added Wong.

Kingston’s ATA Flash card technology emulates an IDE compatible disk drive, but unlike a typical disk drive, this smaller, lighter, noiseless, shock-resistant, semiconductor device has no moving parts. Based on a Type II PC Card form factor using NAND flash memory technology, the Kingston 112MB ATA Flash card is the ideal choice for a rugged, high-performance, portable data storage device. ATA Flash technology is designed for a broad range of PC Card, ATA compatible products, including handheld PCs and digital cameras. Because of its small size and ease of use, Kingston’s ATA Flash cards can go places where a conventional disk drive simply cannot.

Kingston’s ATA Flash card, part #: (DP-ATA/112), has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $440.00. All Kingston’s Flash memory products carry a lifetime warranty and free technical support.
