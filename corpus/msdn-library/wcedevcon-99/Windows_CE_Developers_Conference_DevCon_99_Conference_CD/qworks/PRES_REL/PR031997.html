Kingston Announces QWorks: Free CD-ROM
Reference Tool for Networking VARs

Fountain Valley, CA—(March 19, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, announced today the release of QWorks, a CD-ROM based comprehensive reference tool designed specifically for sales, marketing and technical personnel in the channel. QWorks delivers over 600 megabytes of networking information to Kingston VARs and distributors in an interactive, updatable format. Kingston provides the tool absolutely free to networking professionals in the channel.

With QWorks, sales personnel can analyze product specifications for Kingston’s line of Networking products; generate quotes and proposals; and study networking terms and architectures all through the QWorks easy to use graphical user interface. QWorks contains the text, graphics and logos that marketing professionals require to create advertisements, direct mail, fax sheets and related marketing materials. Additionally, QWorks puts installation manuals, software patches from Microsoft and Novell, and configuration notes at the fingertips of technical support representatives and system engineers.

QWorks is compatible with any CD-ROM-equipped PC running Windows 3.x, Windows 95 or Windows NT. Single user installation takes about five minutes although QWorks may also be installed on a network server for multiple user access.

"Much of the information found in QWorks is not directly related to Kingston products," said Ron Seide, General Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit. "Rather, QWorks is meant to be a comprehensive reference of Local Area Networking information. We want networking professionals in the channel to look to Kingston as not just a manufacturer, but an information provider."

QWorks is available at no charge to Kingston Resellers and VAR partners. For a free copy of the CD-ROM, please contact the Kingston Networking Business Unit at (800) 435-2620.

With sales of more than $1 billion in 1996, Kingston Technology Company is the recognized industry leader in memory upgrades for PCs, workstations and laser printers, worldwide. The formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. The firm markets its product through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology Company became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
