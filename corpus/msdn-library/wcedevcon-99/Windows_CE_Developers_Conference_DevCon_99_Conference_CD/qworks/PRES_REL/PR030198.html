Kingston Networking Products Division
Now Shipping a New Family of Switches

Fountain Valley, CA (March 1, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, announced the release of a new family of switch products. Their new switch line will allow small-to-medium size businesses, with existing Ethernet networks, to migrate to Fast Ethernet both easily and cost effectively.

"With the rapid growth of today’s networks, comes the ever growing demand for bandwidth at both workgroup and backbone locations," said Chris Wong, Product Manager, Networking Products Division. "Kingston's new switches will enable users to substantially increase bandwidth thereby eliminating data gridlock," added Wong. "In addition, the switches act as a bridge linking the 10Mbps network to a 100Mbps network thus allowing for a seamless migration to Fast Ethernet Technology. Once the Fast Ethernet network is fully implemented, Kingston's switches continue their functionality by overcoming the cascading limits of the 100Mbps network to allow the network to grow without limits."

To attain maximum performance and reliability, Kingston's EtheRx switches utilize Store and Forward technology that allows all packets of information being transmitted to be read and stored completely before being delivered. This ensures that all packets are checked for errors and only transferred if they are error-free to their appropriate port.

Adding to its features, Kingston's new switches offer Auto Negotiation and Full Duplexing capabilities. Full Duplexing allows for simultaneous sending and receiving of data enabling an aggregate bandwidth of up to 200Mbps per port. To further ease the traffic of data transmission, the switches partition the network by segmenting each port separately. This process isolates traffic within a packets own segment leaving other segments of the network unaffected by its transmission and thereby increasing throughput and balancing workload. These switches are easy to install and require no hardware configuration.

The KNS200/R is a 2-port 10/100 Mbps switch with a Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $399.00. The KNS208/R and KNS216/R have eight and sixteen ports respectively with two 10/100TX uplink ports. The KNS208/R has a MSRP of $899.00 and the KNS216/R retails for $1,499.00. All of Kingston’s switches undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a three year warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’ staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the US and Canada.
