Kingston Peripheral Products Division Partners with Adelphia Communications As Exclusive Networking Hardware Provider

Leading Cable ISP, Adelphia Communications, Chooses Kingston’s 10/100 Networking Adapters for 2-way Cable Modems

Fountain Valley, CA - (February 3, 1999), Kingston’s Peripheral Products Division announced a new partnership with Adelphia Communications, the seventh largest cable company in the United States. Adelphia will use Kingston’s Dual Speed PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter (KNE100TX) with all of their 2-way cable modem installations.

"As Internet access via cable modem becomes the wave of the future, Kingston is excited to be partnered with Adelphia," says Kevin Flora, manager of Internet access accounts, Kingston. "For the past several months, Kingston has been planning to enter the Ethernet portion of the cable modem equation and selected Adelphia based on their reputation as one of the industry leaders," added Flora.

"Adelphia chose Kingston’s adapters from a number of networking manufacturers after extensive testing in our labs. We found that Kingston’s 10/100 adapter was the most reliable and cost-effective product on the market," offered Ron Vought, manager of cable modem operations, Adelphia Communications. "The ease of installation was of particular importance to us," added Vought. "The time saved by having all the drivers built into the operating system can be spent on additional installations. Also, Kingston’s high level of service allows us to better support our customers."

Anticipating the release of the new DOCSIS specifications, Adelphia selected an adapter capable of both 10Mbps and 100Mbps operation. This enables a customer to maintain their existing hardware while upgrading to future DOCSIS service which will support a downstream speed of up to 36Mbps. By utilizing a 10/100 adapter, a customer is able to take advantage of the maximum speed possible while allowing for future growth in cable modem technology.
