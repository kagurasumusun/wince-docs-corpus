Kingston Networking Products Division
Announces New 16-Port Stackable Fast Ethernet Hub

Fountain Valley, CA - (July 24, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, today announced the addition of a new 16-port stackable Fast Ethernet hub to its family of 100Mbps networking products. Networking professionals are finding Fast Ethernet to be one of the best solutions when facing today’s need to increase bandwidth and ease gridlock.

"Kingston’s networking products offer an affordable, effective solution to this migration/bandwidth issue," said Chris Wong, Networking Product Manager, Kingston. "All of Kingston’s networking products are designed for ease of use and are competitively priced for the medium to large business," Wong continued.

The new 16-port hub features stackability, previously a feature only available in full size 19" racks, in a workgroup form factor to allow smaller networks to have the same growth potential as their larger counterparts. The hub allows three units to be stacked and viewed by the network as one logical unit. This permits for future expandability of up to 48 ports.

In addition to stackability, the Kingston 16-port hub features a switch on the last UTP port for crossover or straight-through cable wiring. This allows the user to cascade hubs without the use of a special crossover cable. The hub is housed in an all-steel form factor for durability and convenient installation.

The KNE16TX/SE has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $559.00. Kingston’s new stackable Fast Ethernet hubs undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the US and Canada.
