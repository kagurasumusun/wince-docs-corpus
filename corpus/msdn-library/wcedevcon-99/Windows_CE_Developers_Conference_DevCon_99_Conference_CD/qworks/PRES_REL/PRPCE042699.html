Kingston’s Peripheral Products Division Slashes Prices As Much As 45%

Prices Cut On Majority of Ethernet, Fast Ethernet,
Flash Memory and Processor Upgrade Products

Fountain Valley, Calif- (April 26, 1999) Kingston® Technology Company today announced it has implemented a drastic price drop on majority of its networking, Flash memory and processor upgrade product lines – some by as much as 45%. Demonstrating Kingston’s commitment to the peripherals market, customers can now take full advantage of quality peripheral products at an even better price.

"Through higher sales volume and our engineers’ relentless pursuit of product cost reduction, we are now able to bring peripheral products to market at very aggressive prices without compromising quality or our industry-recognized high level of service," said Chris Wong, product manager, Peripherals Division, Kingston. "This new pricing gives resellers a competitive edge while maintaining a healthy profit margin," added Wong.

The price drop is a sequential step in the restructuring of Kingston’s Networking Division to expand their product offering to a full line of peripheral products designed to bring the customer a complete computing solution. Kingston’s Peripheral line now includes processor upgrades, flash memory, and networking products. Kingston is also looking to add other peripheral products to the line in the near future.

Users that have never considered networking their systems can now take advantage of utilizing Kingston’s peripheral products at a fraction of the cost. To find out how to more about Kingston’s peripheral products, visit Kingston online at www.kingston.com. Kingston peripheral products come with a limited warranty and free technical support.
