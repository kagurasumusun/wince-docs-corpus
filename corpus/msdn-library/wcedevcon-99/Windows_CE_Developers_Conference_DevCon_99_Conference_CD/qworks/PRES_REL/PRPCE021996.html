Kingston uses AMD’s Am5x86tm Microprocessor
in New TurboChiptm 133 Upgrade

AUSTIN, TX-- (February 19, 1996), Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) today announced that Kingston Technology Corporation will use the Am5x86 microprocessor to power its new fifth generation upgrade product. The TurboChip 133 increases performance of most 486SX, DX or DX2-based systems by more than 250 percent of the original processor.

TurboChip is a chip-for-chip replacement upgrade, meaning a user simply removes the existing 486 microprocessor and inserts the TurboChip into the vacant processor socket. In addition to the Am5x86 microprocessor on board, TurboChip 133 includes a 5- to 3.45-volt voltage regulator and a cooling fan, providing end users with an easy and affordable way to upgrade to Pentium 75MHz-plus performance.

"The TurboChip 133 showcases the power of the Am5x86 microprocessor," said Rob Herb, director of marketing for AMD’s Personal Computer Products Division. "Kingston is providing an ideal product for end users who want 586 performance from their 486 systems."

"TurboChip™ 133 provides Pentium 75-plus processing performance for a large installed-base of 486 systems that do not employ the OverDrive socket," commented Ray Wang, Kingston’s PC Enhancements Product Manager. "Users now can affordably upgrade their systems’ hardware capabilities to meet the demands of today’s software technology without sacrificing any original computing configuration."

The Am5x86 microprocessor outperforms a Pentium 75 device in industry-standard benchmark tests, delivering fifth-generation performance while taking advantage of existing 486 system designs and infrastructure. It is manufactured using AMD’s sixth-generation 0.35-micron process technology. A 16-Kbyte write-back cache, along with an integrated floating point unit (FPU), increases the performance of the Am5x86 device’s core.

Availability and Pricing

TurboChip 133 will be available for shipment in late-February 1996. The product will carry a manufacturer’s suggested retail price (MSRP) of $139 each.
