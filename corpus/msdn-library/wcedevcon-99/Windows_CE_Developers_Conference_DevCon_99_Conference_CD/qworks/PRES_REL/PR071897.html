Kingston Technology Networking Products
Chosen #1 Among Canadian Resellers

Fountain Valley, CA—(July 18, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer was recently awarded with Computer Dealer News’ Annual Reseller Choice Award. The award, initially presented to Kingston at Comdex Canada (7/10 - 7/12) represented top honors in the Networking Interface Card (NIC) category.

"Kingston rolls out the ‘CARPET’ for its resellers. Carpet defines our unit’s philosophy in the network interface market," said Ron Seide, General Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit. "Compatibility, Availability, Reliability, Price, Easy installation and Technical support," commented Seide. Five of the eight criterion for the Reseller Choice Awards were actually part of Kingston’s philosophy.

Competing in an Evans Research survey of 400 Canadian Resellers with networking giants such as Intel, SMC and 3Com, Kingston received the highest scores in three of the categories, second in another and well above average in the fifth. These high marks further underscore Kingston’s dedication to providing the best networking solutions for its chain of network resellers.

Kingston’s networking interface cards are 100% tested and backed by a lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified Netware Engineers. Technical support is toll free in both the U.S. and Canada.

With over $1 billion in annual sales for 1996, Kingston Technology is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, workstations, notebooks, servers and printers, worldwide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
