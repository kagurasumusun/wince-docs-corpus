Kingston Chosen As Ethernet Adapter Provider By Prime Services Group for DSL Solutions

Fountain Valley, CA - (February 2, 1999), Prime Services Group, a California corporation specializing in high quality voice, data and fiber solutions, has chosen Kingston Networking as their ethernet products provider for business and residential solutions. The demand for increased bandwidth and Internet access in the data and telecommunications industries are prime examples of the rapid changes taking place in the technological field in the last decade. Kingston is proud to be partnered with Prime Services Group (PSG) whose well-developed infrastructure has positioned them as one of the leaders in the high speed Internet access industry.

"Prime Services Group chose Kingston Networking from a number of Networking vendors after extensively testing their products both in the lab and in the field. It is our goal to help our clients maximize their technological investments by doing the job right the first time," says Bob Chipley, President of PSG. "Kingston’s reliability and ease of installation help us to achieve that goal," added Chipley.

One of Prime Services Group’s primary customers, Pacific Bell, is forecasting one million new DSL service installations for major California metropolitan areas in 1999. PSG has partnered with Pacific Bell to provide premise equipment installation and support. To meet these needs, Kingston’s PCI 10/100 adapters will be used where an adapter is required. To ensure customer satisfaction and product reliability after installation, all Kingston networking products are backed by an extensive warranty and toll-free technical support.
