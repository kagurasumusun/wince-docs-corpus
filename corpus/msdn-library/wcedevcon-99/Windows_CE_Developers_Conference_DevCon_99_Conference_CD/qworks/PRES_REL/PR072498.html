Kingston Networking Products Division Expands
Canadian Distribution with GB Micro Canada

Fountain Valley, CA--(July 24, 1998), Kingston Technology announced today the signing of a new Canadian distributor, GB Micro, to sell Kingston’s growing line of networking products. Effective immediately, GB Micro will become the newest channel partner, joining quality Kingston distributors including Ingram Micro, Merisel, Multi-Micro and Tech Data.

"Providing our customers with reliable service and quality products is our main focus," said Leif Chiu, Canadian Account Manager of Kingston’s Networking Products Division. "A key component to our strategy is to establish strong distribution partnerships."

"GB Micro’s sees a future in networking products--a contender for major growth. Kingston has a trusted name in the memory industry and by working with GB Micro, it is an ideal situation for our Networking Products Divsion. The partnership with the Networking Division was a logical and welcomed step for both Kingston and GB Micro."

Kingston Technology is the world’s largest independent manufacturer of memory products for servers, workstations, desktops, portables, and electronic devices and is one of the fastest growing contract manufacturers to DRAM makers and PC OEMs. Over the last ten years, Kingston has diversified its product lines to include processor upgrades, flash memory, networking hardware, and storage products. With strictly regulated ISO facilities in the United States (ISO 9001), Ireland (ISO 9002), and Taiwan (ISO 9002), Kingston markets its products through an extensive worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers.
