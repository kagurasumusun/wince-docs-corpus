  Kingston Improves Ethernet Hubs:
Adds Lifetime Warranty

Fountain Valley, CA— (May 31, 1996), Kingston Technology Corporation, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, announced today a new improvement to Kingston’s popular Workgroup Ethernet hubs: the addition of a limited lifetime warranty.

The EtheRx family of Ethernet hubs presently includes an integrated cross-over port on the Kingston EtheRx 8-port and 16-port Workgroup Ethernet hubs, while the EtheRx 8-port stackable hub allows the user to purchase as few as 8-ports at a time and provides the flexibility of stacking up to three additional 8-port stackable hubs to create a single logical 32-port repeater. All three designs also incorporate bi-color LEDs which indicate not only link/activity and collision, but also turn a solid yellow color when a port is under jabber lock and auto partition.

"Kingston customers have been very pleased with the rugged, all-steel construction and compact design of our Ethernet hubs," explained Jason Jacobi, Product Marketing Manager. "The addition of a lifetime warranty demonstrates our faith in the high quality, cost-effective design of our Ethernet hubs." This new, limited lifetime warranty is part of the Kingston plan to bring the power of distributed computing to the large majority of small businesses that are not currently networked and is yet another example of Kingston’s commitment to supporting their network customers.

With sales in excess of $1.2 billion for 1995, Kingston Technology Corporation is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, workstations, servers and laser printers. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology Corporation became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
