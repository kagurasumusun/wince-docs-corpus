Kingston Improves Stackable Ethernet Hubs

Fountain Valley, CA—(May 23, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, announced today the immediate availability of two new 8- and 16-port enhanced versions of their Stackable Ethernet hubs. The products feature improved diagnostic and line utilization LED displays for ease of network management.

The enhanced stackable EtheRx hubs include bar graph LED’s which indicate collisions and utilization as a percentage of segment bandwidth. "The improved line utilization LED’s provide management at a glance which enables quick and easy evaluation of bandwidth constraints by the network administrator," commented Kevin Sherman, Marketing Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit. "This will make the future management of small to medium size networks far less costly and much easier to implement than SNMP solutions," Sherman continued.

The IEEE 10BASE-T Ethernet standard allows for up to four hubs or similar repeating devices on a single network segment, limiting the LAN administrator’s options as an Ethernet network expands. When hubs are stacked, rather than cascaded, the network "sees" these multiple stacked hubs as a single hub. Consequently, when adding a new hub to a growing network by stacking it with an existing hub, the new hub will not count toward the four repeater limitation of Ethernet. Up to five Kingston hubs, and as many as 80 ports, may be stacked together.

In addition to stackability, the products feature a built in cross-over switch on the last UTP port for connecting to a workstation or cascading another Ethernet hub. This allows users to cascade hubs by simply switching the crossover port to the cascade position, enabling the connection of additional Ethernet hubs without the use of a special crossover cable. The products are housed in an all steel, small footprint chassis for durability and convenient installation.

Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price, MSRP, for the 8-port version is $165 and the 16- port version carries a retail price of $349.

The EtheRx Stackable Ethernet hubs are subject to the extensive Kingston 100% testing policy and are backed by a lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified Netware Engineers. Technical support is toll free in both the US and Canada.

With over $1 billion in annual sales for 1996, Kingston Technology is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, workstations, notebooks, servers and laser printers. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers.
