KINGSTON EXPANDS PC CARD STORAGE CAPACITY TO 2GB
WITH RELEASE OF NEW DATAPAK 1GB

Fountain Valley, CA--(March, 1999), Kingston Technology, a leading supplier of PC Card peripherals, today announced the first 2GB PC Card hard disk storage upgrade with the release of DataPak 1GB, a PC Card Type III compliant hard disk drive with a native storage capacity of one (uncompressed) Gigabyte.

With the release of DataPak 1GB, Kingston addresses the growing need for higher capacity, high performance, and more rugged hard disk drives in today’s mobile computing environment. Kingston’s DataPak 1GB is compatible with most conventional disk compression utilities and can achieve the break-through 2 GB storage capacity with a 2:1 compression ratio. Additionally, Kingston’s DataPak 1GB incorporates 128KB dual-ported FIFO data with caching and look-ahead technology to perform at an average seek time of 12ms with a maximum transfer rate of up to 20 megabytes per second.

According to Ray Wang, PC Enhancement Product Manager at Kingston, "DataPak 1GB is the largest PC Card storage device available today. With the proliferation of PC Card slots and DataPak's large storage capacity, users can actually utilize their desktop personal computers without toting them along while traveling or working remotely."

The Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price, (MSRP), for DataPak 1GB is $799. "The introduction of DataPak 1GB marks the first time that the price for PC Card storage falls to approximately $1.30 per megabyte," said an exuberant Wang. "We are committed to serving the market and look forward to seeing the trend of higher capacity and lower costs continue."

DataPak 1GB consumes a mere 10 milli-amps of power in both sleep and standby mode, and only 410 milli-amps in access mode. "With this low power draw feature, DataPak offers users the valuable advantage of longer battery life for their portable computers," added Wang. "Now, users can get more accomplished while traveling."

The newest member to the DataPak family of portable storage solutions, DataPak 1GB utilizes the many advantages of the PC Card standard, including portability, security and hot swapping. DataPak 1GB has compact physical dimensions of (H-.41", L=3.37", W=2.13"); allowing for quick and easy data transporting between systems.

With many of today’s portable systems costing upward of $3,000, DataPak is an ideal solution for corporations employing the pool sharing technique for their portable systems. Users can run the application software off the system hard drive and use DataPak for storing individual files--saving the cost of purchasing multiple systems.

As with all Kingston products, each DataPak is thoroughly tested prior to shipping to ensure full reliability and compatibility. Included with the DataPak is a handy protective pouch for further protection during transportation. To accommodate systems not equipped with a peripheral eject feature, DataPak is packaged with pull-tabs for easy removal. Kingston guarantees the DataPak 1GB hard disk drives with a one-year warranty and offers free technical support.
