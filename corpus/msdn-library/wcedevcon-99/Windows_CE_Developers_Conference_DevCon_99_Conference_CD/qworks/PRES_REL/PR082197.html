Kingston Technology Increases Networking
Distribution Strength in New Zealand

Fountain Valley, CA—(August 21, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer announced today that Tech Pacific, the leading New Zealand distributor of computer products, will distribute the full range of Kingston PC and Apple networking products.

"Combining a high quality product at an extremely attractive price the Kingston networking product line is a perfect fit with Tech Pacific’s current line of computer hardware,"stated Richard Harri, Tech Pacific Product Manager. "Dealers may now purchase Kingston 10/100 EtheRx PCI adapters for around $80.00, allowing exceptional margins in a competitive market,"continued Harri.

"Tech Pacific has an extremely effective marketing and sales strategy to which Kingston can provide the perfect complement," according to Mike Huang, Kingston Asia Pacific Account Manager. "With Tech Pacific’s strong reputation for customer service and support we look forward to a successful partnership,"continued Huang.

With over $1 billion in annual sales for 1996, Kingston Technology is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, workstations, notebooks, servers and laser printers, worldwide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
