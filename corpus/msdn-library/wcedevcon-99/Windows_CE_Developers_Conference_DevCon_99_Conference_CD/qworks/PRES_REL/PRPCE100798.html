Kingston Technology Partners with MobilePlanet
With 4MB CompactFlash Web-based Promotion

Free Kingston 4MB CompactFlash Card Now Available with Special
Mobile Planet Web Purchases

Fountain Valley, CA – (October 7, 1998) Kingston Technology Company today announced a special promotion offering a free Kingston 4MB CompactFlash card in conjunction with the sale of Windows CE-based Handheld and Palm-size PCs for MobilePlanet. Now running, the promotion allows any user purchasing Windows CE-based Handheld or Palm-size PCs from any of MobilePlanet’s web sites to receive a free Kingston 4MB CompactFlash card.

"This offer allows users of Windows CE-based Handheld PCs and Palmsize PCs to expand their memory capability and take advantage of all the functionality that the unit has to offer," said David Kwan, Product Manager, Kingston. "Our partnering with MobilePlanet we continue to demonstrate our dedication to the Flash memory market," added Kwan.

"Microsoft is pleased by this special offer from Kingston and MobilePlanet of CompactFlash cards for devices powered by the Windows CE operating system," said Diana Heileman at Microsoft. "The CompactFlash card feature allows users of Palm-size PCs and most Handheld PC models to take advantage of extra memory to further enhance their mobile computing experience."

The promotion, valid while supplies last, will also offer a Kingston 8MB CompactFlash card for $29.95 if the user wishes to opt for an upgrade with higher memory capacity than the 4MB CompactFlash card. Users can take advantage of this offer by visiting the MobilePlanet web site at www.mobileplanet.com, by visiting Microsoft at www.microsoft.com/windowsce/mplanet.asp or by calling 1-(800)-MPLANET.
