Kingston Cuts Ethernet Hub Prices

Fountain Valley, CA— (July 5, 1996), In a move to strengthen its position as the low cost leader of high quality PC and Mac compatible Ethernet hubs, Kingston Technology Corporation announced they are slashing reseller prices on their popular EtheRx family of rugged steel 8-port, 8-port stackable and 16-port Ethernet hubs to $89, $109 and $169 respectively. To help resellers maintain strong margins MSRP price will remain unchanged.

"When we released our EtheRx Ethernet hubs a year ago, our efficient manufacturing allowed us to put a lot of downward pressure on competing hub prices. Over the last year, our competitors have been adjusting their margins to meet that downward pressure," explained Jason Jacobi, Product Marketing Manager. "We have continued to develop even more efficient ways of manufacturing, allowing us to further lower our prices and still offer the uncompromised compatibility, reliability and ease of use that Kingston Networking resellers have come to expect."

This new, lower pricing brings the company one step closer in its pursuit to bring the power of distributed computing to the large majority of small businesses who are not currently networked and depend on Value Added Resellers to formulate, implement and maintain their information infrastructure.

The EtheRx family of Ethernet hubs carry a lifetime warranty and include either integrated cross-over ports for cascading or proprietary stacking ports. All three designs incorporate bi-color LEDs which indicate not only link/activity and collision, but also turn a solid yellow color when a port is under jabber lock and auto partition. A BNC port is included on all versions for connection to a thin Ethernet backbone.

With sales of $1.3 billion for 1995, Kingston Technology Corporation is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, workstations, servers and laser printers. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers.
