Fast EtheRx 10/100 Mbps Dual Speed Workgroup Hub

Release date: July, 1998

 

The Kingston Fast EtheRx dual-speed Workgroup Fast Ethernet hubs are Class compliant repeaters ideal for the small-office / home-office environment. The dual-speed 10/100BASE-TX Workgroup hubs conform to IEEE802.3u 100BASE-TX, IEEE802.3i 10BASE-T, and IEEE802.3 CSMA/CD standards. The KND800TX offers eight (8) UTP ports that auto-negotiate 10/100Mbps operation and a cascade switch that allows the last UTP port on the hub to support both crossover and straight-through cable wiring. Ideal for inter-connecting 10BASE-T Ethernet networks with 100BASE-TX Fast Ethernet networks, the KND800TX can also be used as a simple 10BASE-T or 100BASE-TX repeater. For easy troubleshooting, the front panel includes a variety of LEDs, including Power, Collision, 100TX, Link, and Activity. Compact in size, the EtheRx Workgroup hubs are easy to install and require no hardware configuration.
