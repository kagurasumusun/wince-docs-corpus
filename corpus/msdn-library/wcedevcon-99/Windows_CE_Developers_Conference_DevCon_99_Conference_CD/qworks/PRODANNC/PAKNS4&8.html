Fast EtheRx 10/100 Mbps Switches

Release date: July, 1998

 

Kingston’s Fast EtheRx 10/100Mbps Fast Ethernet switches are high-performance, rack-mountable switching hubs ideal for the small-to-medium business. Model KNS800/R supports up to 4K MAC addresses per port, for a total maximum of 32K MAC addresses and the KNS400/R supports up to 8K MAC addresses. Kingston's Fast EtheRx switches provide automatic switching between 10Mbps and 100Mbps operation. Each port supports auto-negotiation of 10Mbps or 100Mbps operation, thus allowing standard Ethernet networks to communicate with Fast Ethernet networks, and to segment both 10Mbps and 100Mbps networks. Store-and-Forward approach to switching is used to minimize re-transmission of faulty packets, provide dedicated service, and guaranteed bandwidth for both 10Mbps and 100Mbps segments.
