How would you like to check your calendar, look up a phone number, read your e-mail, check on the status of your customer order all with the push of a button on your pocket organizer? Even better, what if all the information was kept up to date with one phone call? Now you can!

What is IBM Mobile Connect?The era of truly mobile computing has finally arrived. Now mobile professionals (include sales people, inspectors, doctors, auditors, meter readers, field service technicians, realtors, executives and insurance claims adjusters), can keep in touch with the vital information they need while away from the office using IBM Mobile Connect.IBM Mobile Connect is an end to end solution that can be used by corporations to successfully deploy vital information to mobile professionals. Specifically, organizations are now able to deliver line of business applications to a hand held device .

  • allowing up to date detailed information to be always at hand
  • allowing data to be validated at the point of creation
  • avoiding transcription errors
IBM Mobile Connect provides direct server access to Lotus Domino and Microsoft Exchange servers from a wide range of hand held devices, sometimes referred to as companion devices, based on 3Com Palm Computing Platform or Microsoft Windows CE.  With IBM Mobile Connect, you can access and perform updates with
  • e-mail
  • calendar
  • address book
  • to-do list
  • memos
  • database
  • new software
  • back-up-recovery
Improved ProductivityMobile professionals turn on their companion devices between 4 and 10 times per day for calendar, addresses, phone numbers and notes. Now they can keep all the information up to date without having to get back to the desktop PC or carry your laptop.IBM Mobile Connect enables cost-effective companion devices to have access to corporate systems, e-mail, intranets, synchronization of data, forms and information to less expensive hand held computing devices so prevalent in today's market. These devices are lighter in weight, less expensive to own and operate, have longer battery life and "instant on" capabilities. One can receive and respond to e-mail without a "long booting" of your laptop and access forms from your companion devices. All of these features increase productivity of mobile workers.Increased Customer SatisfactionDeploying IBM Mobile Connect can increase customer satisfaction by arming your mobile customer contact people with vital information related to their customers. Customers will perceive faster responsiveness, higher credibility and more value in the relationship. Convenient and Efficient ConnectionIBM Mobile Connect makes it easy for a mobile professional to conduct his or her business by providing a menu of options on the companion device. These are the simple steps for the mobile professional to take:select the appropriate tasks (e.g. inbox or calendar) via the menu push the "Connect" buttonIBM Mobile Connect will thenconnect to the network,perform the selected tasks, anddisconnect. Instant Access Where NeededIf you can make a phone call on your phone (wireless or wireline), you can connect to your e-mail and corporate systems and easily synchronize your companion device for easy access. IBM Mobile Connect works with many mobile phone compatible with Palm or Windows CE hand held device -- just add a cable and IBM Mobile Connect Client Software and your up and running. It also works with wireline modems compatible with the devices.Using Standard ComponentsIBM Mobile Connect uses de facto standard (and leading) hardware platforms and software components. It interacts with industry applications such as Lotus Domino and Microsoft Exchange servers. The connectivity of IBM Mobile Connect is via industry standard TCP/IP protocol with the PPP authentication protocol. The client devices supported by IBM Mobile Connect are today's Microsoft Windows CE 2.0 and PalmOS 2.0+ capable devices. The server component runs on Microsoft Windows NT 4.0.
