The Multifunctional Platforms
   

The multifunctional platform combines the sturdiness of dedicated hardware solutions with the flexibil-ity of the PC world. The multifunctional platform will be a new and innovative category of products - a mixture of classic components (e.g., operator panels and PLCs) and the PC. The products of this category - the multi panels (MP) - are based on Microsoft's Windows® CE and, like operator panels, can be used to control and monitor machines on site. In addition to visualization, these devices will even be able to handle other automation tasks multifunctionally. Together with Windows CE V3.0, a SIMATIC soft PLC will also be available.

Multi panel MP270
 
Starting immediately, the MP270 SIMATIC multipanel offers an introduction to the new category of products. Since Windows CE's diskless operating system makes it particularly suitable for rugged industrial environments, an MP270 can also be used to control and monitor machines in applications in which the vibration resistance required and the presence of large amounts of dust previously restricted the use of a PC. HMI functionality is primarily that of the PC version ProTool/Pro.
