SPONSORS



Xircom, Inc., is the world's leading manufacturer and OEM supplier of mobile communications solutions, including Integrated PC Card and PC Card products, which connect portable computer users to corporate networks, the Internet and other online resources. The Company's products are sold and supported in over 100 countries through a global network of distributors, integrators and resellers. Xircom, headquartered in Thousand Oaks, Calif., with regional headquarter offices in Antwerp, Belgium, Tokyo, Japan and Singapore, has 1100 employees worldwide.
