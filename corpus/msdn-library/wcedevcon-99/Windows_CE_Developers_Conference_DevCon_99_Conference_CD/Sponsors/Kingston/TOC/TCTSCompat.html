TurboChip is compatible with a very broad base of systems.  If a particular manufacturer or model is not shown on one of our compatibility lists that does not mean that that make or model is incompatible.  It simply means that we have not received a verified report from either a customer or our in-house testing facility that confirms that the system is compatible. 

If a system falls into the class of systems that a particular TurboChip was designed for our policy is to consider that system compatible unless proven otherwise.  These compatibility lists are composed of systems that we or our customers have tested with the TurboChip and have found to be compatible.  These lists are constantly changing, are not considered complete and are provided as such for your convenience.
