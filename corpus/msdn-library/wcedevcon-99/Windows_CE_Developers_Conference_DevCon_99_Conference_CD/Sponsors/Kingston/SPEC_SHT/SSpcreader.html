PC Card Readers

 

Kingston's line of PC card readers are the ideal way to bridge the gap between your desktop PC and flash compatible devices. Just plug your reader into your PC's parallel port (printer port) and you're ready to go! The small size of Kingston's ATA and CF Card Readers allow you to easily bring your digital images and data with you. Simply click-and-drag files across your desktop under Windows 95, 98 or NT platforms.
