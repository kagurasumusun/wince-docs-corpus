Kingston Technology Announces
EtheRx Workgroup Series 8-Port Stackable Hub

Fountain Valley, CA- (February 1, 1995), Kingston Technology, a world-wide leader in networking products, today announced the upcoming release of the EtheRx Workgroup Series 8-Port Stackable Hub.

The Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub allows smaller networks to now enjoy the flexibility and cost-effectiveness of 10Base-T wiring and carries an aggressive Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $199.00. Utilizing the Stackable Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub, a small workgroup setup can use unshielded twisted pair (UTP), wiring, commonly known as telephone wire, to transmit Ethernet data signals. Network management and troubleshooting are made easier with the 10Base-T wiring since the Ethernet workgroups are connected in a star topology, focusing the cabling on a single concentrator, or hub. Networks employing the EtheRx Stackable Hub can stack up to four hubs, connecting them to form a single 32-Port logical unit.

"Kingston is committed to providing the best quality networking products at the best possible price," said Ron Seide, Kingston’s Networking Business Unit General Manager. "Our goal is to make networking available to everyone. Making 10Base-T networking affordable at the workgroup level is an example of this commitment."

As 10Base-T Ethernet NIC card prices continue to drop below $100.00 retail, many small to mid-size companies will be encouraged to save time and money by connecting systems together, allowing the sharing of data and resources, like printers and modems.

With a cost per port of less than $25.00, the switch to 10Base-T cabling becomes more attractive to workgroups instead of the more bulky coaxial systems. Workstations installed with coaxial cabling that choose not to switch to 10Base-T cabling may still be connected to a new 10Base-T network via the BNC port provided by the 8-Port Hub.

Shipping now, the EtheRx Stackable Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub carries Kingston’s five year warranty and free technical support. The EtheRx Stackable Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub is the latest addition to Kingston’s innovative line of quality networking solutions.

Concurrent with the release of the EtheRx Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub are two additional products: the recently released Workgroup Series 8-Port Hub and the TokenRx PCMCIA Token-Ring Adapter. Other networking products offered by Kingston include the Micro Channel and ISA TokenRx Dual Interface Adapter Cards and two Multistation Access Units, the EtheRx line of ISA, PCMCIA and EISA Ethernet Local Area Network cards, 10Base-T Network Hubs, Transceivers and a Dual Interface Pocket Ethernet Adapter.

With sales over $800 million in 1994, Kingston Technology is the recognized leader in memory, processor, networking and storage upgrades for PCs, workstations and laser printers. In a recent Gallup poll, Kingston was recognized as the leading independent manufacturer of accelerator and memory products to the Fortune 1000. The firm markets its products through a world-wide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers.
