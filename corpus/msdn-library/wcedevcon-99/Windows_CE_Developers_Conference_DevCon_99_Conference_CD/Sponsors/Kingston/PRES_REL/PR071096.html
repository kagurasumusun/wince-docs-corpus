Kingston Technology Announces
EtheRx 10/100 Mbps Fast Ethernet Adapters

Fountain Valley, CA— (July 10, 1996), Kingston Technology Corporation, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, today announced the introduction of the EtheRx PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet Adapter. The new EtheRx PCI 10/100 Ethernet adapter is fully compliant with current PCI Local Bus Rev. 2.0 and IEEE 802.3, 802.3i and 802.3u specifications. The adapters carry an attractive Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price, (MSRP), starting as low as $139.00, and offer full support of Windows 95 and Windows NT.

The EtheRx PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter features N-Way Auto-Negotiation which automatically configures the adapter for 10Mbps or 100Mbps operation depending on the current line speed. The bus-mastering design optimizes throughput while maintaining low CPU utilization. Additionally, QStart, Kingston’s unique GUI, configuration and diagnostic program, makes Network Operating System configuration virtually automatic. The EtheRx PCI 10/100 adapter allows for maximum flexibility: 10Mbps operation today and 100Mbps upgradability ready for the future.

"At Kingston, we’ve dedicated ourselves to using cost-efficient manufacturing to provide Ethernet hardware that offers unprecedented pricing and value. Our popular EtheRx family of certified, high performance PCI, ISA and PC Card Ethernet adapters represent the first manifestation of this strategy," explained Ron Seide, General Manager of Kingston’s Networking Business Unit. "With the EtheRx PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter, we’re simply extending our strategy by supporting the increasingly popular Fast Ethernet Standard," Seide added.

Based on the industry-leading Digital Semiconductor 21140 10/100Mbps PCI Ethernet controller, the EtheRx PCI 10/100 offers unmatched hardware compatibility. To make the migration to Fast Ethernet seamless, Kingston ships the EtheRx PCI 10/100 with a complete suite of drivers: ODI for DOS, Novell Server Drivers, NDIS2 for DOS, NDIS2 for OS/2, NDIS3, Packet Drivers and SCO LLI drivers. Certification is pending for all popular Network Operating Systems including Novell Netware and Microsoft Windows 95 and Windows NT.

Each EtheRx PCI 10/100 is individually tested and is backed by a lifetime warranty. Staffed by Certified Netware Engineers, technical support is comprehensive and provided free of charge. Additionally, technical support is toll-free in the United States and Canada. The EtheRx PCI 10/100 is available for immediate delivery.

With sales of $1.3 billion for 1995, Kingston Technology Corporation is the recognized industry leader in memory upgrades for PCs, workstations, servers and laser printers, worldwide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. The firm markets its product through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology Corporation became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
