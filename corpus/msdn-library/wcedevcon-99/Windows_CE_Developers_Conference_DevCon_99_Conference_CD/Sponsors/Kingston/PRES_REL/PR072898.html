Kingston Networking Offers
New 10/100 Fast Ethernet Adapter
New Adapter Manufactured with Kingston Chipset Sports Aggressive Pricing

Fountain Valley, CA – (July 28, 1998), Kingston Technology, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, today announced the addition of a new 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter manufactured with Kingston’s own chipset. By implementing its own chipset, Kingston is able to offer this new adapter for $69.00 -- a fraction of the price of standard 10/100 cards that typically retail for $100.00 or higher.

"After being in the Fast Ethernet market for some time now, we feel we have enough experience to provide a chipset that can match the performance of industry standard adapters at a substantial savings," commented Chris Wong, product manager, Networking Products Division, Kingston. "Providing a cost-effective solution to our customer’s needs is always our first concern," said Wong.

Kingston’s new 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter, part #: (KNE110TX), is a 32-bit, high performance bus-mastering PCI adapter that complies with PCI Local Bus Specification. For easy recognition of network status and trouble-shooting, the Fast Ethernet adapter includes a variety of diagnostic LED’s including Link, Activity, 100TX Speed Detection, and Full-duplex status. The adapter supports most Network Operating Systems such as Microsoftâ , Novellâ , LANtasticâ , Banyanâ , IBMâ and SUNâ .

The new Kingston KNE110TX has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price, (MSRP), of $69.00. Kingston’s new Fast Ethernet adapters are certified by most major network operating systems, undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified NetWare Engineers in both the United States and Canada.
