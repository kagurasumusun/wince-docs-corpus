Kingston Technology Announces New 24-Port Fast Ethernet,
Dual Speed Hubs

Fountain Valley, CA -- (November 3, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, today launched two new hubs: a 24-Port Fast Ethernet Hub and a 24-Port Dual Speed Networking Hub. Fast Ethernet and Dual Speed Hubs provide two solutions to assist in increasing bandwidth capabilites and easing network gridlock.

"With more companies turning to Fast Ethernet to solve their data bottlenecks, there is a growing need for a single tool to accommodate both 10Mbps and 100Mbps," said Chris Wong, Product Manager, Kingston Peripheral Products Division. "Kingston simplifies the process, thereby allowing the transition to Fast Ethernet to be gradual and cost-effective," added Wong.

Kingston’s 24-port Fast Ethernet Hub features stackability in a workgroup form factor to allow smaller networks to have the same growth potential as their larger counterparts. The new hub allows three units to be stacked and viewed by the network as one logical unit. This feature permits for future expandability of up to 72 ports. In addition to stackability, the product features a switch on the last UTP port for crossover or straight-through cable wiring.

Kingston’s 24-port Dual Speed Hub has 24 ports with 10/100 auto-sensing capability on every port. Built-in switching, which routes network traffic according to speed, allows for easy communication between 10Mbps and 100Mbps. The ability to mix and match any combination of 10Mbps and 100Mbps devices on the same hub allows for a gradual and smooth transition to Fast Ethernet technology.

The KNE24TX/RS 24-port Fast Ethernet Hub has a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $809.00. The KND2410TX 24-port Dual Speed Hub has a suggested list of $999.00. Kingston’s new 24-port hubs undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a limited lifetime warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’s staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the U.S. and Canada.
