Kingston, Adaptec and Symantec Team Up To Enhance Your Enterprise. Special Show Prizes Available to N+I Attendees

Networld + Interop ‘97, Las Vegas, NV--(May 6, 1997), Working in tandem with Adaptec Corporation and Symantec Corporation, Kingston Technology, (Booth #4283, Fountain Valley, CA), the world’s leading independent manufacturer of memory products, announced today a special partnering event where Networld+Interop show attendees can learn how to enhance their enterprise as well as win valuable combinations of software/hardware prizes at N+I this week.

During the course of the show, attendees may obtain a special co-logoed button at either Adaptec, Kingston or Symantec’s booths. When spotted by special partnering representatives, the button-wearing attendee will be awarded with a certificate for a first level prize, redeemable at Kingston’s booth.

Once the winning wearer obtains his or her initial prize they are then eligible to go onto the grand prize round, which will be held in the form of a drawing at the end of each day. Grand Prizes include: one 32MB Kingston Desktop RAM, one Kingston 8-Port SOHO Ethernet Hub, two EtheRx Client PCI Ethernet Adapters, one Kingston Data Express Removable Drive Enclosure, one Adaptec AAA - 131 PCI-Wide Ultra SCSI Array Adapter Kit and one software title from Symantec. The names of the two, daily grand prize winners will be posted at the Kingston show booth and on Kingston’s web site, www.kingston.com, each day.

This collaboration reinforces the commitment of Kingston, Adaptec and Symantec to offer complementary technologies which benefit mutual customers. "It’s a fun and informative program that makes sense," explained Gary MacDonald, vice president of marketing for Kingston. "Memory is a natural to enhance the many capabilities of just about any software. Users can witness an overall performance increase with all of their applications any time they increase the amount of memory in their system. By providing our networking and storage products for the drawing, we can help N+I attendees learn how to enhance their overall computing experience," continued MacDonald.  Utilizing the integral technologies of the three companies, N+I attendees can see for themselves the many benefits offered to enhance their enterprise computing goals.

"Historically, entry-level server users have settled for embedded network operating system Redundant Array of Inexpensive Disks (RAID) software because high-end RAID storage systems did not meet the price/performance requirements for this server segment," said Roger Cox, marketing manager for Adaptec’s Mission Critical Subsystems business unit."Now, the Adaptec AAA-130 Series array adapters combined with the Kingston Data Express removable drive enclosures provides a higher-performance, more robust fault-tolerant RAID solution that meets the pricing structure of all entry-level servers."

To meet the needs of its customers, Kingston offers more than 2,200 memory products that support the diverse needs of over 30 major brands of personal computers, workstations, printers, scanners, and other electronic devices with specific memory requirements. Using a just-in-time manufacturing model, Kingston designs memory modules to the requirements of the individual machine and manufacturer to ensure the memory is completely compatible with the system’s applications and operating systems. To ensure complete reliability, every memory product is individually tested prior to shipping and is backed by Kingston’s 24-hour exchange policy.

About Adaptec

Adaptec provides bandwidth management technologies for organizations building the global information infrastructure. Its high performance I/O, connectivity, and network products are incorporated into the systems and products of major computer and peripheral manufacturers. Founded in 1981 and headquartered in Milpitas, Calif., Adaptec (NASDAQ:ADPT) employs 3000 people worldwide in design, manufacturing, sales, service and distribution.
