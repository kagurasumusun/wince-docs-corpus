Kingston Unveils Industry’s Highest Performing Processor Upgrade

New TurboChip® 366 is First Complete Processor Upgrade
To Offer 366MHz AMD-K6®-2 Processor with 3DNow!ä Technology

Fountain Valley, CA—April 5, 1999, Kingston® Technology Company today announced the release of TurboChip® 366, Kingston’s most powerful processor upgrade to date based on the AMD K6-2 microprocessor with 3DNow! technology.

The latest addition to Kingston’s award-winning line of processor upgrades, TurboChip 366 is a chip-for-chip replacement solution that enables users of 166MHz or higher AMD-K6 and Pentium-based systems to take advantage of today’s technology without replacing their entire system.

Kingston’s TurboChip family of products allows system users to increase performance by as much as 280% over their existing processor without the cost and complexity involved in replacing the motherboard and other components. Upgrading is quick and easy; no additional changes to the hardware or software are required. In a matter of a few minutes, a system user may transform a system by replacing the existing socket 5 or socket 7 system CPU with Kingston’s TurboChip 366 upgrade.

"Rapid advancements in both hardware and software can create a real problem for users wanting to keep up with current technologies but not in a position to replace their personal computing systems so frequently," said Chris Wong, product manager, Kingston. "The new TurboChip 366 will allow individuals, small offices, government agencies, schools and large organizations to take advantage of today’s leading applications while prolonging the life of their existing system investments," added Wong.

The new TurboChip 366 utilizes 3DNow! technology and DirectX 6, delivering the utmost in online performance, vivid graphics and realistic imagery. "The AMD-K6-2 processor enables a new generation of high-performance desktop systems that advance the state of the art in 3D and multimedia computing," said Dave Sheffler, AMD’s, vice president of sales and marketing for the Americas. "The 3DNow! technology-optimized transform, clipping and lighting operations of DirectX 6.0 will provide performance boosts for Kingston’s TurboChip end-user customers, whether it’s at home or higher performance corporate IT environments."

"With the TurboChip family of upgrades, Kingston is the first to address CPU upgrade compatibility across the board," continued Wong. "Kingston offers users low-cost processor upgrade options from leading microprocessor manufacturers including both AMD and Intel."

TurboChip 366 will be available in April 1999 and carry a suggested list price of $249. The complete upgrade package comes with a heat sink/fan integrated cooling unit, a pass-through power cord and 2.2 V to 3.3 V voltage regulator. The product is offered through Kingston’s traditional channel of personal computer distributors and resellers. Kingston’s web site, www.kingston.com has detailed product information and purchase referrals for customers interested in the new TurboChip 366 processor upgrade solution. A detailed list of compatible systems is also listed on the Kingston web site.
