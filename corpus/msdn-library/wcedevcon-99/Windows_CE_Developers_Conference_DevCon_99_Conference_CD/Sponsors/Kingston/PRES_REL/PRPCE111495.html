Kingston Expands PC Card Storage Family
With Release of DataPak 340

Fountain Valley, CA-- (November 14, 1995), Kingston Technology, a leading supplier of innovative PC Card storage technology, today announced the release of DataPak 340, the latest addition to Kingston’s line of PC Card hard disk drives. Premiering at Fall COMDEX ‘95, DataPak 340 is a PC Card Type III compliant hard disk drive, boasting a native storage capacity of 340 megabytes. This follows the April 1995 release of DataPak 260, the first high capacity PC Card hard drive ever released.

With the release of DataPak 340, Kingston addresses the demand for increased capacity, high performance, durable and affordable portable hard disk drives in today’s mobile computing environment. DataPak’s hard disk drives endure up to 750Gs of non-operating shock, made possible by a proprietary dynamic ramp loading technology. This technology ensures that the read/write heads never come in contact with the recording surface of the disk, thus eliminating failures due to head slap and heads sticking to the disk. In addition, DataPak drives have an average seek time of just 10.6ms and a media transfer rate of up to 5.7 megabytes per second.

According to Kingston Product Manager Raymond Wang, DataPak 340 is a major break-through in the 1.8 inch form factor storage technology. "With the introduction of DataPak 340, we are able to provide PC Card hard disk drives that have the storage capacity users require at a price that makes portable computing effective and affordable, since users can extend the life of a portable system using DataPak," commented Wang.

The DataPak family features the lowest power consumption among PC Card hard drives on the market. DataPaks draw a mere .005 watts of power in sleep and standby mode, and 1.3 watts when accessed.

The DataPak family brings the many advantages of the PC Card standard to portable users, including: portability, security and plug-and-play. DataPak comes in a compact design with physical dimensions of .41" high x 2.1" wide x 3.3" long, (10.4mm x 53.3mm x 83.8mm), allowing for quick and easy data transporting amongst systems. DataPak ships with SystemSoftâ Card and Socket Services and is compatible with most PC Card Type III compliant systems, including Apple PowerBooks. DataPak is also compatible with Windows 95.

DataPak is an ideal solution for corporations choosing to employ the ‘pool sharing’ technique for a staff using a limited number of desktop or portable systems. Individual users can simply run their application software off the system’s hard drive and use DataPak for storing user files, saving the cost of purchasing multiple systems.

As with all Kingston products, each DataPak is thoroughly tested for full reliability and compatibility. Included with the DataPak is a handy pouch for further protection during transportation. To accommodate systems not equipped with a peripheral eject feature, DataPak is packaged with pull tabs for easy removal.

DataPaks are backed by a one year warranty. Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) is $595 for the 340 MB version and $485 for the 260 MB version.

During its eight years, Kingston has diversified its product lines to offer processor upgrades, networking hardware and storage products.

Kingston markets its products through a world-wide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. Kingston is ISO 9001 certified as part of a continuing global effort to assure customers with the highest levels of both quality products and service.
