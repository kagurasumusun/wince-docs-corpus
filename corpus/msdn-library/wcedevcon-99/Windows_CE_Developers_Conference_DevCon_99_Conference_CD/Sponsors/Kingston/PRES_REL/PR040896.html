Kingston Improves Ethernet Hubs
Adds Integrated Cross-Over Ports for Easy Expansion

Fountain Valley, CA— (April 8, 1996), Kingston Technology Corporation, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, announced today that the firm has again improved the design of their small office Workgroup Ethernet hubs. The new design includes an integrated cross-over port on the Kingston EtheRx 8- and 16-port models. The new design also incorporates bi-color LEDs which indicate link/activity, collision and turn solid yellow when a port is under jabber lock and auto partition.

"We decided to improve the hubs, which were already high quality, cost-effective products, as a result of input we received from our reseller/integrator partners. They were happy with the rugged, all-steel construction and small foot print," explained Product Marketing Manager Jason Jacobi, "Yet they expressed concern about the need to build in expansion capabilities for the growing small office, home office, (SOHO), networks."

The addition of the integrated cross-over port means it is not necessary to manufacture, or use, special 10BASE-T cross-over cable to connect Kingston EtheRx hubs to additional hubs when "daisy chained" as a network grows. "Now by simply flipping a switch, the network integrator can use straight through 10BASE-T cable. This saves a considerable amount of time and eliminates possible difficulties of improper cross-over cable manufacturing," added Jacobi.

For Kingston Technology Corporation, the design improvement is another step in the pursuit of bringing the power of distributed computing to the large majority of small businesses across the country that are not currently networked. Kingston users and resellers can feel secure, knowing that they are getting the same uncompromised level of compatibility, reliability and ease of installation of higher cost competitors, without the higher price.

With sales over $1.2 billion for 1995, Kingston Technology Corporation is the recognized industry leader in memory products for PCs, portables, servers, workstations and laser printers worldwide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its product through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology Corporation became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
