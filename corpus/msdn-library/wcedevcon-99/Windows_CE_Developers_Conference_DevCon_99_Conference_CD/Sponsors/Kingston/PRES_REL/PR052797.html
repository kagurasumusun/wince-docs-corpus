New Kingston Stackable Fast Ethernet Hubs
Extend Network Expandability

Fountain Valley, CA—(May 27, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer announced today the immediate availability of 8- and 12-port stackable Fast Ethernet hubs. The new hubs feature unique stack in and stack out ports that allow as many as six hubs, and up to 60 ports to be configured as a single logical unit.

Stackability is a more important issue for users of Fast Ethernet than standard Ethernet. Unlike standard Ethernet, which allows for up to four hubs or similar repeating devices on a single network segment, Fast Ethernet allows for only two hubs. This can limit the LAN administrator’s options as a Fast Ethernet network expands. When hubs are stacked, rather than cascaded, the network "sees" these multiple stacked hubs as a single hub. By stacking a new hub with with an existing hub, when adding on to a growing network, the new hub will not count toward the two repeater limitation of Fast Ethernet.

In addition to stackability, the products feature dual connectors on last UTP port for crossover or straight-through cable wiring. This allows the user to cascade hubs without the use of a special crossover cable. The products are housed in an all steel rack mount form factor for durability and convenient installation.

"As 100mps Ethernet becomes more of a standard in today’s market, Kingston maintains its commitment to supporting the needs of the user," commented Kevin Sherman, Marketing Manager, Kingston Networking Business Unit. "By offering a stackable, rack-mounted hub as well as our existing 100mps PCI adapters, Kingston offers a total Fast Ethernet solution," continued Sherman.
