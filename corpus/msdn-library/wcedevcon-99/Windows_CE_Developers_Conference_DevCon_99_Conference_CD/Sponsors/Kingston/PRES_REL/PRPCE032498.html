Kingston Unveils Industry’s Highest Performing Processor Upgrade

New TurboChip® 233 is First Complete Processor Upgrade on the Market
To Offer 233 MHz Pentium® Processor

Fountain Valley, CA—March 24, 1998, Kingston Technology Company today announced the release of TurboChip® 233, Kingston’s most powerful processor upgrade to date, and the only upgrade package on the market based on the Intel 233 Mhz Pentium processor with MMX™ technology.

The latest addition to Kingston’s award-winning line of processor upgrades, the TurboChip 233 is a chip-for-chip upgrade package that enables users of Pentium-based systems to quickly and easily replace their existing socket 5 or socket 7 system CPU with a 233MHz Pentium® with MMX™ processor upgrade based on the Intel microprocessor technology.

Kingston's TurboChip family of products provides users an inexpensive and easy way to double the performance of an existing system without the cost and complexity involved with replacing the motherboard and other components. To perform an upgrade, users simply remove the existing CPU from the motherboard and replace it with Kingston’s TurboChip. No additional hardware or software changes are required. Expected to ship in April, TurboChip 233 will carry a suggested list price of $299.

"Rapid advancements in both hardware and software can create a real problem with users who need to keep up with current technologies but cannot afford to replace their PCs on a regular basis," said John Sutherland, director of new product development, Kingston. "Products like the new TurboChip 233 allow individuals, small offices, government agencies, schools and other organizations to take advantage of today’s leading applications while prolonging the life of their existing systems."

The 233 MHz Pentium processor with MMX™ technology contains performance-enhancing features including Intel Superscalar Architecture, Intel MMX™ technology and multiprocessor support. Kingston’s TurboChip 233 integrates a 3.5x clock mulitiplier and has an onboard voltage regulator that enables older systems to seamlessly work with the new CPU.

"With this latest version of TurboChip, Kingston is the first to address CPU upgrade compatibility across the board," said Raymond Wang, product manager, Kingston. "Kingston offers users a family of low-cost processor upgrade options from leading microprocessor manufacturers including Intel and AMD. Since this latest solution is based on Intel’s Pentium processor with MMX technology, compatibility is guaranteed for systems using the Intel chipset."
