Kingston Networking Products Division
Reduces Prices Up To 32%

Fountain Valley, CA--(January 22, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of quality networking hardware, is ringing in the new year with an implementation of major price reductions -- some by as much as 32 percent. The decision for the reduction marks the firm’s strong commitment to providing reliable networking products at the best price possible.

"Through higher sales volume and our engineers’ relentless pursuit of product cost reduction, we are now able to bring products to market at a very aggressive price without compromising quality or our industry-recognized high level of service," said Chris Wong, networking product manager. "The new pricing gives resellers a competitive edge while maintaining a healthy profit margin," added Wong. Of the products included in the price reduction, Kingston’s EtheRx Fast Ethernet 100TX PCI adapter was reduced from a Manufacturer’s Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $109 to the new low of only $89.00. The Kingston 12-port Fast Ethernet hub was reduced from the suggested retail of $1,499.00 to $1,099.00, and the SOHO 5-port Ethernet hub dropped from $99.00 to $79.00.

About Kingston

Kingston Technology is the world's largest manufacturer of memory products for servers, workstations, desktops, portables, peripherals, and other equipment with manufacturing facilities in the United States, Ireland and Taiwan. Over the last ten years, Kingston has diversified its product lines to offer processor upgrades, networking hardware, and storage products. Kingston markets its products through an extensive worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. Kingston has been continuously ISO 9001 certified since 1994.
