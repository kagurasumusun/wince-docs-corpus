Kingston Wins Novell Approval As Newest Authorized Test Facility

Fountain Valley, CA— (December 21, 1995), Kingston Technology Corporation, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer, announced today that the firm has been approved as an Authorized Novell Certification Alliance Test facility. Novell’s Approval validates Kingston Technology Corporation as an adapter and hub manufacturer with the ability to properly develop Novell drivers and support Novell Network Operating Systems.

The approval is the latest result in a series of strategic initiatives on the part of the Kingston’s Networking Business Unit and is expected to shorten Kingston’s product development cycle significantly, allowing the firm to do a majority of the certification in house, thus reducing the time required to obtain driver certification.

"Partnering with Novell as an authorized Certification Facility is a prime example of Kingston’s commitment to offering the highest possible level of compatibility and reliability," explains Jason Jacobi, Product Marketing Manager. "We are taking similar steps with other important Network software developers, including Microsoft, SCO, Digital Equipment Corporation and Banyan Vines."

Achieving the Novell approval is another step in Kingston’s pursuit of bringing the power of distributed computing to the large majority of small businesses that are not currently networked. Kingston’s aim is to provide users and resellers alike with the security that they are getting the same uncompromised level of compatibility, reliability and ease of installation of higher cost competitors.

With annual sales of $1.3 billion for 1995, Kingston Technology Corporation is the recognized industry leader in memory upgrades for PC’s workstations and laser printers world-wide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. The firm markets its product through a world-wide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology Corporation became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
