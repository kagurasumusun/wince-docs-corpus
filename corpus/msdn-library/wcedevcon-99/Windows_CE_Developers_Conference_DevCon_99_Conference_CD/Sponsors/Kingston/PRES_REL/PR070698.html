Kingston Networking Products Division
Expands Their Family of Switch Products

Fountain Valley, CA (July 6, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, today announced the expansion of their switch product line to include two new dual speed switches. Available in both four and eight dedicated, auto-sensing 10/100 ports, these switches allow for a flexible and cost-effective migration to Fast Ethernet technology. No hardware configuration is required making installation fast and easy.

"All of Kingston’s switches utilize a store-and-forward technology for transferring packets of information at maximum wire speed," said Chris Wong, Networking Product Manager, Kingston. "This means that each packet is completely checked for errors before it is delivered to its destination, thereby eliminating the transfer of faulty packets. Store-and-forward technology assures both reliability and efficiency," continued Wong.

Full duplexing is another characteristic of Kingston’s switches, which make them a valuable asset to any network. The typical shared ethernet network utilizes half-duplexing, which means that a workstation may either transmit or receive data, but cannot do both at the same time. When connected directly to a file server or workstation, full duplexing allows for transmission and receiving to occur simultaneously thereby allowing throughput of up to 200Mbps on a 100Mbps connection with zero collisions.

The 4-port 10/100 Mbps dual speed switch, part #: (KNS400/R) has a Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $699.00. The 8-port 10/100 Mbps dual speed switch, part #: (KNS800/R) has a retail price of $1,249.00.

All of Kingston’s switches undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a three year warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’ staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the US and Canada.
