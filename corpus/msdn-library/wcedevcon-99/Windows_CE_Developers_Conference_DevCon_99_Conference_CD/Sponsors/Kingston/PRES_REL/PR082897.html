Kingston Technology Committed to Supporting the Mac Market

Fountain Valley, CA—(August 28, 1997), Kingston Technology Company, the leading manufacturer of memory products and growing networking hardware manufacturer announced today the release of Macintosh Powerbook drivers for the Kingston PCMCIA Ethernet Adapter KNE-PC2, adding to the existing list of products supporting Apple Macintosh machines. The latest Mac driver set includes both a classic networking and Open Transport driver for maximum support of the Macintosh Powerbook series of machines.

The classic networking driver, for use with PC Card Manager V2.0, supports Powerbooks such as the 190, 1400 and 5300 series machines which use or emulate the older Motorola sixty-eight thousand series processors. The Open Transport driver, for use with PC Card Manager 3.0, supports Powerbook 2400 and 3400 series machines which are PowerPC based units. "Having had Macintosh Open Transport support for both our 10mbps KNE40 PCI Ethernet adapter as well as our KNE100TX Fast Ethernet adapter for some time, Kingston further demonstrated it’s commitment to the Macintosh user with support for the portable line of Mac products,"commented Kevin Sherman, Marketing Manager of Kingston Networking Business Unit. "Kingston networking now provides Macintosh support for both portable and desktop users, providing the ideal solution for any configuration,"continued Sherman.

In addition, the new KNE-PC2 Macintosh driver ships with the Kingston Installer which will determine the driver type needed, classic networking or Open Transport, and includes a diagnostic application to provide adapter transmit and receive statistics and run a send/receive test on the adapter itself. With the Kingston Installer, the user can install the correct driver and test the adapter automatically or elect to use a custom installation procedure offering the flexibility to meet the particular demands and expertise of the user.

With over $1 billion in annual sales for 1996, Kingston Technology is the recognized industry leader in memory products forPCs, workstations, notebooks, servers and laser printers, worldwide. The recent formation of the Kingston Networking Unit marks the manufacturer’s successful entry into the Ethernet market. Kingston markets its products through a worldwide network of distributors, major reseller chains and independent dealers. As a continuing demonstration of its commitment to service and quality on a global level, Kingston Technology became ISO 9001 certified in 1994.
