Kingston Networking Products Division Announces
New Family of EtheRx Dual Speed Hubs

Fountain Valley, CA (July 22, 1998), Kingston Technology®, the growing manufacturer of networking hardware, today announced the immediate availability of a new line of dual speed hubs.

"With more companies turning to Fast Ethernet to solve their data bottlenecks, there is a large need for a single tool to accommodate both 10Mbps and 100Mbps devices, said Chris Wong, Networking Product Manager, Kingston. "Dual speed hubs make communication between the two technologies simple, thereby allowing the transition to Fast Ethernet be gradual and cost-effective," added Wong.

Kingston’s EtheRx Dual speed Fast Ethernet hubs have eight or sixteen 10/100 auto-sensing ports. Built in switching, which routes network traffic according to speed, allows for easy communication between 10Mbps and 100Mbps. The ability to mix and match any combination of 10Mbps and 100Mbps devices on the same hub allows for a gradual and smooth migration to Fast Ethernet. The option to stack or cascade the hubs provides the option for future growth.

The 8-port 10/100 workgroup hub, part #: (KND800TX) has a Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) of $449.00. The 8-port 10/100 stackable hub, part #: (KND810TX), has the ability to stack up to four units as one logical unit, and has a MSRP of $519.00. The 16-port 10/100 stackable hub, part #: (KND1610TX), also has stacking capability and retails for $769.00.

All of Kingston’s hubs undergo extensive 100% testing and are backed by a three year warranty. Additionally, technical support is available free of charge from Kingston’ staff of Certified Netware Engineers in both the US and Canada.
