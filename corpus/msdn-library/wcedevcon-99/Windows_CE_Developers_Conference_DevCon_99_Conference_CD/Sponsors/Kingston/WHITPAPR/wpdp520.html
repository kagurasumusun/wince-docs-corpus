Product Position Statement (PPS)

Due to the growing popularity of portable computers, peripheral manufacturers have been challenged to provide a standard expansion slot interface to make it easier for mobile users to add functionality to their portable systems. The Personal Computer Memory Card International Association (PCMCIA) was formed to meet this challenge. The PCMCIA has helped to increase the variety and availability of peripheral devices for portable computers by creating a standard specification.  This standard makes it easier for manufacturers to build products for portable computers.    PCMCIA (a.k.a. PC Card) peripherals such as data/fax modems, memory cards, network interface cards and hard disk drives have helped portable system users come closer to getting desktop-type  performance and functionality from their portable computer.

One of the most important PC Card peripherals is the hard disk drive. Most older portable systems are equipped with 2.5" disk drives. These 2.5" drives are small and lightweight but they are also somewhat limited in capacity.

The DP520 is a Type III compatible PC Card hard disk drive. DataPak conforms to the PC Card standard and makes storage upgrades fast and easy. With its miniature design and large capacity (520MB uncompressed), DataPak 520 is small enough to fit into a shirt pocket and large enough to store an entire suite of applications and files. Multiple DataPaks effectively allow for unlimited storage on PC Card Type III equipped portable systems and provide data transportability to other PC Card compatible systems and digital devices.

Kingston makes these benefits affordable to the average consumer.  Combining high performance and quality, DataPak is the most cost-effective PC Card hard disk drive available today.

With the addition of DataPak 520, Kingston continues to expand its PC Card storage product line. Features and Benefits:

Feature: PC Card Type III compliant.
Benefit: DataPak 520 is compatible with most popular notebook personal computers and digital devices that have a PC Card Type III slot. Kingston has tested DataPak 520 in many of the most popular portable systems available today.
Feature: 520MB of uncompressed and formatted data storage capacity.
Benefit: DataPak 520 is the highest "uncompressed" capacity PC Card compliant hard disk drive available today.   When comparing competitor's products make sure to look at uncompressed capacity.   DataPak 520 is compatible with disk compression utilities and, if desired, can be compressed to create a drive with a capacity of up to 1GB.
Feature: 128KB dual ported FIFO data buffer with cacheing and look-ahead technology.
Benefit: Up to 20MB transfer rate per second with a 12 ms seek time. For transfer rate, higher is better and for seek time, lower is faster. DataPak 520 performance is one of the best in the industry.
Feature: Data backup.
Benefit: Back up your data using standard DOS, OS/2, Windows 95, Windows NT or Macintosh O/S commands to protect against data loss.
Feature: Rugged design to absorb high G-Shock.
Benefit: DataPak 520 can absorb G-shocks of 200G operating and 750G non-operating. This feature protects DataPak from failing under normal transporting and operating conditions.  DataPak's durability provides better protection of your investment and a lower failure rate than standard 2.5" hard disk drives.
Feature: Multi-user system
Benefit: DataPak 520 is ideal for corporations promoting the "pool sharing" technique for portable computer systems. Application software can be stored on the shared system’s original hard disk drive while the data files can be stored on a DataPak 520. Users can remove the DataPak 520 and take their data with them when they have finished using the system.
Feature: Optimum power management
Benefit: DataPak 520 draws only 410 milli-amp of battery power during spin-up and only 10 milli-amp in both stand-by and sleep mode. This low power consumption gives users longer battery life than with other PC Card hard disk drives.
Feature: Padded protective pouch
Benefit: Adds extra protection to DataPak 520 during transport.
Feature: DataPak 520 is a removable media
Benefit: To prevent unauthorized access to sensitive data and to protect investment, simply remove DataPak 520 from the system and secure it in a safe place.  Also, removability effectively allows for unlimited data capacity.
Feature: One year warranty
Benefit: All DataPak 520 hard disk drives come with a one year comprehensive warranty. The DataPak 520 is backed by Kingston’s free unlimited technical support.
