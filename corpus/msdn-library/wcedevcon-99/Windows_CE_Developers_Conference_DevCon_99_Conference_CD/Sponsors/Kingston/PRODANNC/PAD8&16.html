8 and 16-port EtheRx Dual Speed Fast Ethernet Stackable Workgroup Hub

Release date: July, 1998
 

The Kingston Fast EtheRx dual-speed Fast Ethernet stackable hubs are Class compliant repeaters ideal for the small-office / home-office environment. The dual-speed 10/100BASE-TX hubs conform to IEEE802.3u 100BASE-TX, IEEE802.3i 10BASE-T, and IEEE802.3 CSMA/CD standards and are stackable up to a maximum of six (6) hubs. The KND810TX and KND1610TX offer eight (8) or sixteen (16) 10/100Mbps UTP ports that auto-negotiate 10BASE-T and 100BASE-TX operation. Port number 8 has an additional uplink port to support both crossover and straight-through cable wiring. Ideal for inter-connecting 10BASE-T Ethernet networks with 100BASE-TX Fast Ethernet networks, both models can also be used as simple 10BASE-T or 100BASE-TX repeaters. For easy trouble-shooting, the front panel includes a variety of LEDs, including Power, Collision, 100TX, Link, and Activity. Compact in size, the EtheRx dual-speed hubs are easy to install and require no additional hardware configuration.
