Fast EtheRx VP PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet Adapter

Release date: July, 1998

 

The Fast EtheRx VP PCI 10/100 Fast Ethernet adapter is a 32-bit high performance PCI adapter that conforms with PCI Local Bus Specifications and offers Bus-mastering support. Diagnostic LEDs including Link, Activity, 100TX Speed Detection, and Full-duplex status provide easy recognition of network status and trouble-shooting. The Fast EtheRx VP PCI 10/100 adapter is dynamically configured by the computer system’s BIOS for effortless hardware installation.
