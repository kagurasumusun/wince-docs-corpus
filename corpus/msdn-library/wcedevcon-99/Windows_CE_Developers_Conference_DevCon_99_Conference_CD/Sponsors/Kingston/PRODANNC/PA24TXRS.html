Fast EtheRx 24-port Stackable 100Base-TX Hub

Release date: December, 1998
 

Kingston’s Fast EtheRx Stackable 100BASE-TX Fast Ethernet hubs are Class compliant repeaters ideal for the medium to large business environments where 100Base-TX is the standard. The KNE24TX/RS conforms to IEEE802.3u 100BASE-TX standards and can be stacked up to a maximum of six (6) hubs using the Stack-In/ Stack-Out ports on the back of the unit. The Fast EtheRx hub offers twenty-four (24) 100BASE-TX UTP ports for Fast Ethernet connectivity and supports an optional 100Base-FX ST or SC fiber module. The last UTP port on the hub supports both crossover and straight-through cabling. For easy troubleshooting, the front panel includes a variety of LEDs, including Power, Collision, Utilization, Link, Activity, and Partition. Designed to with larger installations in mind, the 19"-Rack-mountable Fast EtheRx 24-port Stackable hubs are easy to install in any industry standard rack and require no additional hardware configuration.
