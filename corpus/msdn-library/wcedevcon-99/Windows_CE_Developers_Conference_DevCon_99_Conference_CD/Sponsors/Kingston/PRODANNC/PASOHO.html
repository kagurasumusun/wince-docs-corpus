EtheRx SoHo Ethernet Hubs

Release date: April, 1997
 

Available in both a 5-port and an 8-port version, Kingston’s EthRx SOHO (Small Office Home Office) Ethernet hubs provide the utmost in affordable, reliable Ethernet connectivity. Their pocket-sized form factor and optional in-line keyboard power cable make the EtheRx SOHO the perfect solution for small offices, home offices and portable computing users. Plus, the EtheRx SOHO’s integrated hub-to-hub crossover port means you will always have room to grow.
