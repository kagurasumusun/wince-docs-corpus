24-port Fast EtheRx Dual Speed Ethernet
Stackable Workgroup Hub

Release date: December, 1998
 

The Kingston 24-port Fast EtheRx dual-speed Fast Ethernet stackable hub is a Class compliant repeater ideal for the medium to large businesses. The dual-speed 10/100BASE-TX hub conforms to IEEE802.3u 100BASE-TX, IEEE802.3i 10BASE-T, and IEEE802.3 CSMA/CD standards and is stackable up to a maximum of four (4) hubs. The KND2410TX has twenty-four (24) 10/100Mbps UTP ports that auto-negotiate 10BASE-T and 100BASE-TX operation. Port number 24 has an additional uplink port to support both crossover and straight-through cable wiring. Ideal for inter-connecting 10BASE-T Ethernet networks with 100BASE-TX Fast Ethernet networks, the KND2410TX can also be used as a simple 10BASE-T or 100BASE-TX repeater. For easy trouble-shooting, the front panel includes a variety of LEDs, including Power, Collision, 100TX, Link, and Activity. Designed to be 19"-Rack-mountable, the KND2410TX fits easily into any wiring closet with an industry standard rack.
