EtheRx Pro Series Stackable Ethernet Hubs

Release date: May, 1997

 

EtheRx Pro Series stackable hubs are the enterprise wide solution for smaller companies and organizations. Stack ‘n PlayTM stacking ports allow as many as 5 hubs, up to 120 ports, to be configured as a single logical unit thereby meeting the demands of growing networks. Smart MonitorTM bar graph LED’s provide at-a-glance the collision and bandwidth utilization data network managers need. Solid steel 19" rack-mount design ensures years of reliable use.
