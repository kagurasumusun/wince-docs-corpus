EtheRx 16-port Fast Ethernet
Stackable Workgroup Hub

Release date: July, 1998

 

Kingston’s Fast EtheRx Workgroup 100BASE-TX Fast Ethernet stackable hubs are Class compliant repeaters ideal for the small-office and home-office environment. The KNE16TX/SE conforms to IEEE802.3u 100BASE-TX standards and can be stacked up to a maximum of three (3) hubs using the Stack-In / Stack-Out ports on the back of the unit. The EtheRx hub offers sixteen 100BASE-TX UTP ports for Fast Ethernet connectivity. The last UTP port on the hub supports both crossover and straight-through cabling. For easy troubleshooting, the front panel includes a variety of LEDs, including Power, Collision, Utilization, Link, Activity, and Partition. Compact in size, the EtheRx Workgroup hubs are easy to install and require no additional hardware configuration.
