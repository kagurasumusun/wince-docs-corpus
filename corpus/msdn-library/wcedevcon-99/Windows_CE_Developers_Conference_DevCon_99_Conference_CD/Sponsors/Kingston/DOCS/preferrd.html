Kingston Networking Preferred Reseller Program:
A Program Designed To Meet Your Needs

The Kingston® Networking Preferred Reseller Program rewards VAR and Integrator partners with special services and incentive programs. Designed with the way you do business in mind, the Preferred Reseller Program offers participants profit increasing opportunities including special sales incentives, priority access to pre-sales and technical support services and product evaluations. This guide covers the Program in detail, and outlines the features that will make selling Kingston Networking easier and more rewarding than ever.

Qualification Requirements:

That is why we have created a straightforward, hassle-free qualification process. Simply complete the online application at www.kingston.com/nwreg and we will enroll you in the Kingston Networking Preferred Reseller Program. Enrolled Partners Enjoy These Benefits

Exclusive Partner Incentives

Participate in exclusive promotions designed to increase your company's profitability. Loyal business partners are rare. At Kingston we believe your ongoing loyalty deserves to be rewarded.

Exclusive Partner Discounts on Demo Units

Before you recommend a hardware product you will want to test it thoroughly in your own lab or network.  Our Partner Discount features up to 60% off on demo units purchased for this purpose. This is an across-the-board discount that applies to all of our networking products not just to a select group.

Free 30 Day Evaluation Product

With our Free 30-day Evaluation Program you are able to offer your customers the opportunity to try the product before they make their purchase. As a Preferred Reseller you will be pre-approved for this evaluation program.  You can use this program as a risk-free way to win new business.  For example, you can install a new hub or switch at a customer's site to demonstrate the advantages of upgrading their network's performance.

Pre-Approved Cross Ships

At Kingston we test every product 100%.  DOAs are extremely rare.  However, If you do come into a situation where you need a part replaced our Pre-Approved Cross Shipping means your new product will ship out immediately.

This is not a complicated process, simply tell us which replacement product you need and in most cases it will be delivered within 24 hours.

Real-time Updates and Pre-released Product Information

As a Preferred Reseller you will have access to our feedback forum where you can offer feedback on proposed products and current products alike. You will also be signed up for our monthly e-mail announcement so you will always have the latest information on Kingston networking products.

Marketing Materials and Collateral Support

Access to Marketing Materials, QWorks™ and the Networking Product Guide are available so that you may learn about new products, technical specifications and software upgrades. To order these materials, please call us at (800) 435-2620 and we’ll send them right out to you.
