Key Topics

Universal Plug and Play: This revolutionary technology will allow Windows CE-powered devices to recognize and interoperate seamlessly and transparently to end-users. One powerful feature of UpnP is its ability to work independently of the PC so devices can communicate with one another in peer scenarios as well as fully networked environments.

Wireless Communications Architecture: The fundamental need for people to access information anytime - and anywhere - has driven many exciting technologies to Windows CE. We will articulate and demonstrate an end-to-end architecture that allows developers to build solutions that tie together personal and corporate data into an integrated solution.

Solution Spotlights: There will be a featured series of technical and business development sessions focused on showcasing Windows CE solutions that are being deployed today.

Windows CE Toolkits: Great technology coupled with great tools produce outstanding results. We will have many sessions focused on how to use the Windows CE Toolkits for the Visual C++® and Visual Basic development systems, as well as the Platform Builder to develop real-world solutions.
