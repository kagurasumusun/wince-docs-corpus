  TRACKS AND BREAKOUT SESSIONS

Listed below are the tracks we will have at this years DevCon. To view the sessions that will be delivered in each of the tracks, click on the track title.

Leveraging Core Technologies and Components
To fully exploit the hardware and software possibilities of Windows® CE, it’s critical to understand the fundamentals of the operating system. In this technically focused track, we’ll deliver core architecture sessions and take an in-depth look at the system’s basic internal workings. We’ll also explore a host of exciting new components, including Universal Plug and Play and DirectX® which allow you to extend your solutions into exciting new opportunities. Sessions in this track are recommended for hardware and software pros, plus anyone interested in maximizing the potential of Windows® CE. Not for the faint-of-heart - this track packs enough technical “meat” to satisfy the most ravenous technical appetite.

Developing Custom Devices, Intelligent Appliances, and Peripherals
This track has been designed especially for engineers and designers who want to acquire a thorough understanding of the process of porting Windows® CE to custom hardware. The track starts with a general overview on the best practices for preparing for a Windows® CE project and then dives rapidly into the underlying mechanisms of the Windows® CE operating system. We will explore in detail, the creation of the bootloader, optimization of the video driver, debugging a device driver, and much more. We will also introduce tools designed to facilitate the adaptation of Windows® CE such as the new version of Platform Builder and tools to facilitate code optimization and code tracing.

Interactive Hands-on Laboratory
This hands-on laboratory track is designed for developers who want an in-depth opportunity to use and manipulate the latest tools and Windows® CE technologies. Each participant will have at his or her disposal a Windows NT® workstation and Windows® CE target. This target, based on the SH-3 microprocessor, has been designed specifically for this track and will demonstrate all aspects of porting Windows® CE to a custom hardware device. Highlights of this track include the demonstration and usage of all new real time features of Windows® CE. Other sessions range from hands-on experience with new and exiting features of Platform Builder, how to use Visual Basic® in the embedded world, how to profile and do code coverage, and to how to create a Web browser! This track is applicable to all engineers and system designers who want the opportunity to acquire a true hands-on feeling of the new features and functionalities of Windows® CE.

Designing Applications for Information Appliances
This track extends coverage to coding techniques involved in the development of great applications for information appliances. Microsoft® Windows® CE development team members will delivery a hard-hitting, comprehensive look at real world development issues such as input methods, the pocket Outlook® Object Model, development languages, efficient use of the SDK, and porting existing Win32® applications. Additional sessions will target development issues applicable to each of the information appliance family members including Auto PC, Palm-size PC, and Handheld PC. This year we are excited to bring you many real world sessions intended to shine a pragmatic spotlight on how custom applications are developed and designed to effectively solve enterprise-level challenges. This is the real deal - be ready to code!

Utilizing Internet Technologies
Enabling people with the power to do what they want, where and when they want to do it means exciting new form-factors and computing scenarios that extend the possibilities for applications, devices, and web content. In this track we cover each of these facets and how they fit together from integrating Internet Explorer technologies into custom devices and applications to authoring and managing content in the exciting new world of information computing. This track promises to give you a first-hand glimpse into the devices and technologies that will shape the way information is delivered in the future.
