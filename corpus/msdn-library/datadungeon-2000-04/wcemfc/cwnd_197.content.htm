CWnd::ShowCaret

This method shows the caret on the screen at the caret’s current position. Once shown, the caret begins flashing automatically.

At a Glance

Header file: Afxwin.h
Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
Complete documentation: Visual C++ documentation

void ShowCaret( );
