CRect::IntersectRect

This method makes a CRect object equal to the intersection of two existing rectangles. The intersection is the largest rectangle contained in both existing rectangles.

At a Glance

Header file: Afxwin.h
Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
Complete documentation: Visual C++ documentation

Syntax

BOOL IntersectRect( LPCRECT lpRect1, LPCRECT lpRect2 );
