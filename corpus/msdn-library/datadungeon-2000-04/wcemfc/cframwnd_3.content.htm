CFrameWnd::ActivateFrame

This method activates and restores the frame window so that it is visible and available to the user. This method is usually called after a non-user interface event such as a DDE, OLE, or other event that may show the frame window or its contents to the user.

At a Glance

Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
