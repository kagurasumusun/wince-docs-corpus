CComboBox::GetLocale

This method retrieves the locale used by the combo box. The locale is used, for example, to determine the sort order of the strings in a sorted combo box.

At a Glance

Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
