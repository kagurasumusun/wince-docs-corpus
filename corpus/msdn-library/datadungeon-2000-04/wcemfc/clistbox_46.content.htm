CListBox::SetTopIndex

This method ensures that a particular list-box item is visible. The system scrolls the list box until either the item specified by nIndex appears at the top of the list box or the maximum scroll range has been reached.

At a Glance

Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
