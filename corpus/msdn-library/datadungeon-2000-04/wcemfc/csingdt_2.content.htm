CSingleDocTemplate::CSingleDocTemplate

This constructor constructs a CSingleDocTemplate object. You dynamically allocate a CSingleDocTemplate object, and pass it to CWinApp::AddDocTemplate from within the CWinApp::InitInstance method of your application class.
