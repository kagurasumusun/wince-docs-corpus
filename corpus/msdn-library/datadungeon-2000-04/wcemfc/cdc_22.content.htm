CDC::ExcludeUpdateRgn

This method prevents drawing within invalid areas of a window by excluding an updated region in the window from the clipping region associated with the CDC object.

At a Glance

Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
