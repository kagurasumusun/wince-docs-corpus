CWnd::OnStyleChanged

This method is called by the framework after the SetWindowLong function has changed one or more of the window's styles.

At a Glance

Platforms: H/PC 2.0, Palm-size PC, H/PC Pro
Windows CE versions: 1.0 and later
Complete documentation: Visual C++ documentation
