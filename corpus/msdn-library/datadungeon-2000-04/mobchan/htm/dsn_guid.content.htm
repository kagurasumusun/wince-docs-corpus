Mobile Channels Design Guidelines

This discussion focuses on special design considerations when creating a Mobile Channels site for the Palm-size PC. For example, Palm-size PC users want information to appear quickly. This results in the implementation of limited, optimized graphics, and navigation schemes better suited to the compact device display.

When you have generated preliminary Web pages, preview the site on your Palm-size PC and make necessary adjustments to improve readability of key graphics and to ensure the site design effectiveness.
