Text Capabilities

When adding text to a mobile channel, consider the following:

  • Tahoma is the only available font.

  • Because italics are difficult to read on a small grayscale display, avoid applying italic tags to small font sizes.

Note   Text always appears in black and the background is always white, even in tables and table cells. Therefore, include such tag and attribute pairings as <BODY BGCOLOR=WHITE LINK=BLACK> for a more accurate rendering of a Palm-size PC display when working or viewing on a standard monitor.
