Windows CE Developer Case Studies

Less than a year after Microsoft introduced the Windows® CE-based Handheld PC, more than 100 leading independent software vendors, independent Hardware vendors, and shareware vendors are demonstrating support for Windows CE by developing more than 250 applications and peripherals. Here are some examples of how applications for Windows CE are being deployed.

Hoechst Marion Roussel
Taking advantage of existing Win32® expertise, developers from Solution Provider Empower Trainers and Consultants, Inc., and Microsoft Consulting Services (MCS) were able to quickly customize an application for the Microsoft® Windows CE-based Handheld PC, providing sales representatives for international pharmaceutical manufacturer Hoechst Marion Roussel (HMR) with more efficient sales tools.

Landware
The long run is what really interests Ken Landau. His firm, LandWare, has been developing, marketing, and distributing quality applications for handheld computing devices for three and a half years, but because the firm is looking five and 10 years into the future, LandWare decided early on to commit to developing applications for the Microsoft® Windows® CE platform. It's a commitment that has paid handsome dividends even in the very short run that the platform has had so far.
