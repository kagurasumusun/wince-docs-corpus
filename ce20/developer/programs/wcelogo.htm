The Designed for Windows® CE Logo Program

The Designed for Microsoft® Windows CE Logo program was developed to help end users easily identify hardware and software products that are compatible with the Microsoft Windows CE operating system. Users can be assured that the products bearing the Windows CE logo will take advantage of the new technologies integrated into this innovative operating system. And you share the advantage of the investment that Microsoft has in these trademarks, as well as the momentum behind the family of Windows-based products. Review the lists of currently logo'd hardware and software.

For hardware and software vendors, displaying this logo means your products can be instantly identified with this exciting new platform which supports a broad range of communications, entertainment, and mobile-computing devices.

Licensing the Logo allows you to use this symbol to concisely communicate a strong quality message to users of the Windows and Windows CE operating systems. What makes the Logo unique among the many logo programs in the industry is that, in order to qualify to carry the Logo, a software or hardware product must meet a high technical standard. The Logo requirements ensure that the product carrying the Logo takes advantage of the key technologies customers find most important. The Logo will effectively position and promote your Windows-based product.
