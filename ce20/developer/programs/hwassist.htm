Hardware Assistance Program

Hardware can be a difficult commodity to come by in the early stages of development, and even when available commercially it can be expensive to purchase each new platform to ensure cross compatibility. So, in conjunction with Microsoft®, VeriTest Incorporated has developed a plan to supply beta and released Windows CE devices for use by qualified independent software developers for testing and finalizing development.

Visit VeriTest's site at: http://www.veritest.com/windowsce/ce_preview.htm

Or send mail to VeriTest at: hpcpreview@veritest.com.
